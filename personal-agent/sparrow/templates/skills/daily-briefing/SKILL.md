# Daily Briefing

1. Call todo_list to get open to-dos.
2. Call list_schedule to see today's reminders.
3. Call memory_search with today's date and with "birthday" to find
   anything special about today.
4. Reply with at most five bullet points, most urgent first, then one
   friendly closing line. If there is nothing, say the day is clear.
