# Chapter 25: Where to Go Next

You now know every major feature of Claude and have 50 workflows to build on. This last chapter shows you how to turn that into a lasting habit, and where to go if you want to do more.

## Your First 30 Days

Most people who get real value from Claude don't use it for big, dramatic tasks. They use it for many small ones, every day, until it becomes automatic. Here's a simple plan.

| Week | Focus | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Writing and reading | Use Claude for at least one email and one document every day |
| 2 | Files and Projects | Create two Projects for ongoing things in your life |
| 3 | Artifacts and documents | Build one tool you'll keep, and make one spreadsheet or deck |
| 4 | Routines | Set up one scheduled task you'll actually read |

At the end of the month, look at your monthly recap (Settings > Reflect), which summarizes how you've used Claude, and ask yourself: which three workflows saved me the most time? Make those skills or scheduled tasks.

## The One Habit to Keep

If you remember nothing else from this book, remember the Finished-Work Formula from Chapter 4: **Result, Reader, Raw material, Rules.** Describe the finished thing you want, who it's for, give Claude what it needs to see, and state the limits. Everything else in this book is built on that.

## If You Want to Build Software: Claude Code

**Claude Code** is Anthropic's tool for building real software by describing what you want: websites, apps, automations and tools bigger than an artifact. You'll find it from the small code icon at the top of Claude's sidebar. It's more technical than everything in this book, but people with no programming background use it every day.

My book *Vibe Coding for Beginners* walks you through it from your first conversation to a tested, published app.

## If You Want to Build Claude into Your Business

Developers use the **Claude API** to put Claude inside their own products, from customer support systems to research tools. If you run a business and want Claude working inside your own systems, that's the path, usually with a developer's help. My book *Building AI Agents with Claude* covers it step by step.
