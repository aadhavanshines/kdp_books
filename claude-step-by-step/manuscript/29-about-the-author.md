# About the Author

Aadhavan Muthurengan writes clear, practical guides to artificial intelligence for everyday readers and working professionals. His books explain how to use AI tools with confidence, step by step, with real examples and safe habits, and without hype. He is the author of the AI Skillup series, including *Prompt Engineering Mastery*, *Vibe Coding for Beginners* and *Building AI Agents with Claude*, and of *Claude AI for Small Business*. To find any of them, search Amazon for the title together with "Aadhavan Muthurengan."

He wrote this book for everyone who has opened Claude, typed a question, and wondered how to get from an impressive answer to finished work.

If this book saved you time, a short review on Amazon helps other readers find it. Thank you.
