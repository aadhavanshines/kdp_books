# Chapter 7: Documents, Spreadsheets and Slide Decks

Claude doesn't just write text in a chat. It can hand you real files: a Word document, an Excel spreadsheet with working formulas, a PowerPoint deck, or a PDF. It can also make documents, slides and designs that live inside Claude, which you can edit, share and download. In 2026 Anthropic made these available on every plan, including Free.

## Two Kinds of Output

| | Files | Docs, Slides and Designs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you get | A .docx, .xlsx, .pptx or .pdf file to download | A document, deck or design inside Claude |
| Best for | Sending to someone, opening in Office, printing | Working on it with Claude, sharing a link |
| How to ask | "Make this as a Word document" or "...as an Excel file" | "Make a slide deck..." or "Create a one-page design..." |
| Editing | Open in Word, Excel or PowerPoint | Ask Claude for changes, or edit in place |

You don't need to memorize this. Ask for what you want in plain words, and say the file type if it matters: "as an Excel file I can download."

## Making a Document

```prompt
Write a one-page welcome guide for new volunteers at Riverside Food Pantry, as a Word document. Include: what to wear, where to park, the shift times (Tuesday and Thursday 4 to 7pm, Saturday 9 to noon), who to ask for (Maria, the volunteer lead), and five safety rules for handling food. Friendly tone, headings, and a short checklist at the end.
```

Claude writes the content, formats it with headings and a checklist, and gives you a file card. Click it to preview, then download it. Open it in Word or Google Docs and it looks like a document a person made: real headings, real bullet points, not a wall of text.

> **Tip:** If your organization has a template or letterhead, attach it and say "use this template." Claude will follow its layout and styles.

## Making a Spreadsheet

Spreadsheets are where file creation saves the most time, because Claude can build the formulas too.

```prompt
Make an Excel budget for our family for 2027. Columns for each month, rows for: rent, utilities, groceries, transport, insurance, kids' activities, eating out, savings, and other. Our take-home pay is $6,200 a month. Put in my estimates (rent 1,850, utilities 240, groceries 900, transport 380, insurance 310, kids 250, eating out 300, savings 600, other 400). Add monthly totals, what's left each month, and a yearly total, all as formulas. Highlight any month where spending is more than income.
```

What you get back is a working spreadsheet: change any number and the totals update, because they're real formulas, not typed-in results. Ask for a chart and Claude adds one.

Ask follow-ups like you would with a document:

- "Add a second sheet that shows each category as a percentage of income."
- "December has $1,200 extra for gifts. Add that and show the effect."
- "Explain each formula in a note so my partner understands it."

## Making a Slide Deck

```prompt
Make a 6-slide deck for a 10-minute talk at our neighborhood association meeting, proposing a monthly street cleanup. Slide 1 title, slide 2 the problem (litter complaints up from 12 to 31 this year), slide 3 the plan (first Saturday each month, 9 to 11am, supplies from the city's free program), slide 4 what we need (8 volunteers, one sign-up sheet), slide 5 benefits, slide 6 how to join. Very few words per slide, big clear headings, and speaker notes for each slide.
```

Claude builds the deck with a layout and visual style, and writes speaker notes so you know what to say. You can view it inside Claude, ask for changes ("make slide 3 a simple timeline"), and download it as a PowerPoint file.

> **Note:** If you have a company look and feel, you can set it as a **design system** from the + menu, so Claude's decks and designs match your colors and fonts. Chapter 13 explains how.

## Making a PDF

When you need something final that nobody should edit, such as a printed flyer, a menu, a form or a certificate, ask for a PDF:

```prompt
Make a printable one-page PDF sign-up sheet for the street cleanup: title, date, a table with 15 rows for name, phone and email, and a note at the bottom saying where to meet.
```

## Editing Files You Already Have

Attach a document, spreadsheet or deck and ask for changes. Claude returns an updated file.

```prompt
Here is our employee handbook. Update the vacation policy section so new staff get 15 days instead of 12, and add a sentence about carrying over up to 5 unused days. Don't change anything else. Give me the updated Word file and a short list of exactly what you changed.
```

The phrase "a short list of exactly what you changed" is worth adding every time. It lets you check the edit in ten seconds.

- Say the file type if it matters: "as an Excel file I can download."
