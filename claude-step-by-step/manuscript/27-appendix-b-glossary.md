# Appendix B: Glossary

**AI assistant:** A program you talk to in plain language that writes, reads, explains and helps with tasks. Claude is one.

**Artifact:** Something Claude makes that opens beside the chat and that you can use, keep or share: a tool, chart, page, game or document (Chapter 8).

**Auto mode:** A permission setting where Claude acts in connected apps without asking each time, except for actions an automated check flags as risky (Chapter 12).

**Chat:** One conversation with Claude. Each chat is saved in Recents unless it's incognito.

**Claude Code:** Anthropic's tool for building software by describing what you want (Chapter 25).

**Connector:** A secure link that lets Claude read from, and sometimes act in, another app such as Gmail, Google Calendar or Google Drive (Chapter 12).

**Context:** Everything Claude can see while answering: your messages, attached files, project files and instructions.

**Cowork:** The name Anthropic first gave to Claude doing multi-step tasks for you. Since September 2026 it's part of everyday Claude (Chapter 14).

**Design system:** Your saved colors, fonts and layout rules, so decks and designs match your brand (Chapter 13).

**Effort:** A setting that controls how long Claude thinks before answering. Higher effort means better answers to hard questions, and more usage (Chapter 2).

**Finished-Work Formula:** This book's four-part method for requests: Result, Reader, Raw material, Rules (Chapter 4).

**Hallucination:** When an AI states something false as if it were true, such as an invented quote or statistic (Chapter 23).

**Incognito:** A chat that stays out of your history, memory and search, and isn't used to train Claude (Chapter 10).

**Instructions:** Text that tells Claude how to behave for every chat, either in your profile or in a Project.

**Manual mode:** A permission setting where Claude asks before each action in a connected app.

**Memory:** Claude's ability to remember lasting facts from your past chats and search earlier conversations. You can view, edit and delete it (Chapter 10).

**Model:** The underlying AI that writes Claude's answers. In late 2026 the choices include Fable, Opus, Sonnet and Haiku (Chapter 2).

**Plugin:** A ready-made bundle of skills, connectors and settings for one area of work (Chapter 13).

**Project:** A workspace with its own instructions, files, memory and scheduled tasks, for anything ongoing (Chapter 9).

**Prompt:** Another word for a request you send to an AI.

**Research:** A Claude feature that searches many sources and writes a cited report in a few minutes (Chapter 11).

**Scheduled task:** A task Claude runs automatically at the times you choose (Chapter 14).

**Skill:** A saved way of working that Claude follows whenever a request matches it (Chapter 13).

**Source:** A web page Claude used for an answer, listed as a link so you can check it.

**Usage limit:** The amount you can use Claude in a few hours and in a week, depending on your plan (Chapter 24).

**Web search:** Claude looking up current information on the internet while answering (Chapter 11).
