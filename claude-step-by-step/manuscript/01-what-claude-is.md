# Part I: Getting Started

# Chapter 1: What Claude Is, and What It Is Not

Before you click anything, it helps to have the right picture in your head. Most frustration with AI assistants comes from expecting the wrong thing: treating Claude like a search engine, like a person who knows you, or like a calculator that is never wrong. It is none of those. This short chapter gives you a more useful picture.

## Claude in One Paragraph

Claude is an AI assistant made by a company called Anthropic. You talk to it in plain language, by typing or speaking, and it answers in plain language. It can write, read, summarize, explain, plan, analyze numbers, look at photos, create documents and spreadsheets, build small interactive tools, search the web, and, when you allow it, work inside apps such as your email and calendar. It learned how language works by studying an enormous amount of text, and it uses that skill to work out a helpful response to whatever you ask.

## The Best Way to Think About Claude

Think of Claude as a **very fast, very well-read assistant on their first day at your job.**

- **Very fast:** It can draft a two-page report in under a minute and read a 100-page PDF in seconds.
- **Very well-read:** It knows a great deal about writing, business, science, history, law, cooking, travel, software and much more.
- **On their first day:** It knows nothing about *you* unless you tell it: your company, your customers, your style, your deadline, what happened in yesterday's meeting. The more you tell it, the better its work gets.

That last point is the key to this whole book. When Claude gives you a generic answer, it is almost never because Claude is not capable. It is because it was missing information that only you have. Chapter 4 gives you a simple formula for handing that information over.

## What Claude Is Not

**It is not always right.** Claude can make mistakes, including stating something false with complete confidence. This is often called a "hallucination." It happens less than it used to, but it still happens, especially with exact numbers, quotes, dates, legal details and very recent events. Chapter 23 shows you how to check work quickly.

**It is not a search engine.** Claude answers mostly from what it learned during training, which stops at a certain date. It searches the web by itself when a question clearly needs current facts, but for news, prices, opening hours or anything that changes, it's worth asking it to check online and show its sources (Chapter 11).

**It is not a person who knows you.** Claude does not see your screen, your files or your email unless you share them or connect an app. With memory turned on, it can remember things from past chats, but you control what it keeps (Chapter 10).

**It is not a professional adviser.** Claude can explain medical, legal and financial topics clearly and help you prepare good questions. It does not replace your doctor, lawyer or accountant, and it will tell you so when it matters.

## A Quick Word on Safety and Privacy

Anthropic publishes clear rules on how it handles your conversations, and you can change several privacy settings yourself, including whether your chats may be used to improve future models. Chapter 23 walks you through them. For now, one simple rule will keep you safe while you learn:

> **Remember:** Don't paste anything into Claude that you would not be comfortable emailing to a trusted colleague outside your company, until you have read Chapter 23 and checked your settings. That means no passwords, no bank card numbers, and no other people's private information.

## Key Points

- Claude is a fast, well-read assistant that knows nothing about you until you tell it.
- Generic answers usually mean missing information, not missing ability.
- Claude can be wrong. Check facts, numbers and anything important.
- For anything current, ask Claude to search the web and show its sources.
- You can do everything in this book in the main Claude app, with no code.
