# About the Author

**Aadhavan Muthurengan** is an automation and artificial intelligence professional with more than 15 years of experience in the automotive industry.

Throughout a career focused on product development and validation, Aadhavan has designed and built a wide range of in-house tools that automate complex engineering workflows, speed up testing, and help teams deliver reliable, high-quality results. Validation engineering teaches a particular habit: don't trust a design until you've tested how it behaves when things go wrong. That habit runs through this book, in which every building block is a model you can run and every claim about its behavior is measured.

Aadhavan is also the author of *Prompt Engineering Mastery*, a beginner-to-advanced guide to getting better results from AI; *Vibe Coding for Beginners*, a guide to building real software with AI coding tools; *Building AI Agents with Claude*, a guide to agents that use tools, remember, and get real work done; *Python Programming Hands-On*, a practical, tested introduction to programming; *How Large Language Models Are Made*, a clear explanation of how today's AI models are built; and *The Coding Interview Playbook*, 120 original coding problems with tested Python solutions.

Thank you for reading *The System Design Interview Playbook*. Good luck with your interviews: think out loud, use your numbers, and enjoy the conversation.
