## Three suggestions
1. **Focus on OMR.** Orders went from 30 to 119 (almost four times), though from a small base. Consider an OMR delivery day or a small delivery-charge offer to keep it growing.
2. **Plan for the quiet months.** November (Rs. 2,04,090) and January were the weakest. Try pre-booked combos or a hamper push in those months, and stock up before the strong months of October and August.
3. **Lean on your best sellers and eggless cakes.** Chocolate truffle brings in far more than any other product (Rs. 9,38,000). Eggless share of cake orders rose from 31.3% to 34.7%, so keep a good eggless range. Website has the lowest rating (4.31), so check delivery and ordering there.
