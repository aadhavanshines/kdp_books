# Amudha's Home Bakes: Customer Policy

## Refunds
- Damaged or wrong items: full refund, or a free replacement, if the customer reports it
  within 24 hours of delivery.
- Cancellations more than 24 hours before the due time: full refund. Less than 24
  hours: 50% refund, because the cake is usually already baked.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI or card) within 3 working days.
- Taste is personal, so we don't refund cakes because they weren't to someone's taste,
  but we always want to hear the feedback.

## Changes to orders
- Flavour, eggless and message changes are free up to 24 hours before the due time.
- Eggless versions cost Rs. 50 extra per kg.

## Allergies
- Our kitchen handles nuts, wheat, eggs and dairy. We cannot promise any product is free
  of these. Any allergic reaction is passed to Amudha immediately.

## Delivery
- Delivery charges: Rs. 60 up to 5 km, Rs. 120 for 5 to 12 km, Rs. 180 for 12 to 25 km.
