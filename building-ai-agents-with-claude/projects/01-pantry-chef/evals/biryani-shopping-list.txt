- Chicken, bone-in curry cut (1.2 kg) (Main ingredient for chicken biryani for 6 people)
- Ghee (100 g) (For frying onions and layering the biryani)
- Fresh mint and coriander leaves (1 bunch each) (For layering and marinade in the biryani)
- Whole spices: cardamom, cloves, cinnamon sticks, bay leaves (For flavouring the rice and giving the biryani its aroma)
- Red chilli powder (For the chicken marinade)
- Lemons (2) (For the marinade and for finishing the biryani)
