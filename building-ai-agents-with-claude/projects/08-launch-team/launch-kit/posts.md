## Notes for the owner (not for posting)

- Prices come from pricing.md. Cost and margin figures are deliberately left out of the public copy.
- Caption 3 says "You asked for more eggless choices". This is based on 47 of 126 survey respondents (37%). The survey was self-selected, so it is a signal and not proof of what all customers want.
- The WhatsApp line "most ordered item that week" refers to Diwali 2025 only (28 hamper orders, 14-21 Oct 2025). It is a single observation. I did not quote an uplift figure, and I did not promise stock will sell out.
- No Diwali 2026 date or order deadline is included because neither file gives one. Add the date and a cut-off before posting. The research suggests starting promotion about a week before Diwali.
- Delivery fees are not quoted in the copy. The research gives Rs 60 (Anna Nagar), Rs 120 (other areas) and Rs 180 (OMR). Delivery charges were a common complaint, especially in OMR, so consider stating the fee clearly when customers order.
