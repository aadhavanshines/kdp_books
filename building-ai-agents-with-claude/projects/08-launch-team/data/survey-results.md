# Customer Survey, September 2026

Amudha's Home Bakes asked customers to fill in a short online survey between 1 and 20
September 2026. 126 people answered: 81 existing customers and 45 people who follow the
bakery on Instagram but have never ordered.

## Where respondents live

| Area | Respondents |
| --- | --- |
| Anna Nagar and nearby | 38 |
| Velachery, Madipakkam, Medavakkam | 29 |
| OMR (Thoraipakkam to Sholinganallur) | 33 |
| Adyar, Besant Nagar | 14 |
| Other | 12 |

## Key findings

- 64% said they would order more often if delivery took less than 90 minutes.
- Of the 33 OMR respondents, 25 said they had tried to order but gave up because the
  delivery charge to OMR (Rs. 180) was too high.
- Eggless cakes were the most requested addition: 47 respondents asked for more eggless
  options.
- Only 9 respondents said they would pay more than Rs. 1,200 for a 1 kg celebration cake.
- Corporate orders came up unprompted: 11 respondents work in OMR IT parks and said their
  offices order desserts for events at least once a month.

## Selected comments

"I live in Sholinganallur and the delivery fee is more than half the price of the cake."

"Please open somewhere closer to OMR, all my office friends want to order."

"Love the rasmalai cake, but I wish there were more eggless choices."
