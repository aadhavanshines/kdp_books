## Notes on figures in the request that do not appear in posts.md

- "47 survey respondents" is not right as stated. The survey had 126 respondents, and 47 of them (37.3%) asked for more eggless options (survey-results.md, research.md s4). posts.md does not quote 47 or 126, only the qualitative "You asked for more eggless choices", which is supported.
- The "28 hampers" figure is the number of orders, not hampers (see row 11).
- Prices are for new hampers. The old Festive hamper sold at a flat Rs 1,600. The posts do not compare to it, so no issue.

## What I changed in posts.md

WhatsApp broadcast, last paragraph:
- Before: "Last Diwali, 28 hampers were ordered between 14 and 21 October, so we suggest ordering early."
- After: "Last Diwali, 40 hampers were ordered between 14 and 21 October, so we suggest ordering early."

No other edits.

VERDICT: FAIL (as submitted: one wrong figure, 28 orders presented as 28 hampers; corrected to 40 in posts.md, and all other prices, contents and counts check out)
