## Caveats (no change made)

- Row 10, "most ordered": this is true by number of orders (28 against 22 for the next product). It is not a statement about units, because order quantity is in kg for cakes and boxes for other items. Wording is safe as long as readers take "ordered" to mean orders.
- Row 10, "festival week": the data window is 8 days (14 to 21 Oct 2025), not 7. This is a loose, harmless phrase. If you want it exact, use "over the Diwali days".
- Last year's Festive hamper (Rs 1600 per unit in orders.csv) is a different product from the 2026 Deepam and Jyothi hampers. The copy does not claim they are the same, and the price is not quoted, so there is no error. Do not add a price comparison without a source.
- Jyothi Eggless: sources only confirm that the brownies are the eggless version. Nothing in the data says the other items (rasmalai cake jar, cookies) are egg-free. The copy only calls the hamper "Jyothi Eggless" and the brownies "eggless", so it makes no unsupported claim. Do not add "everything inside is eggless" without checking with Amudha.
- Survey says nothing about hampers or Diwali. The copy does not claim it does.
