# Should Amudha open a second kitchen, and if so, where?

Yes, but only conditionally, and the best site is OMR (Thoraipakkam). It is the only option that fixes a demand problem: OMR customers are put off by the Rs. 180 delivery fee, and no similar bakery is on OMR. The documents don't show that OMR can reach the roughly Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue needed, and they don't give the savings figure. So I'd treat it as a go only if Amudha can confirm the savings and some pre-committed demand, such as corporate orders.

## Findings

1. The current kitchen is near capacity, so there is a real capacity constraint that a second kitchen would relieve.  
   > "The kitchen in Anna Nagar is running at about 90% of its capacity on weekends. On 14 weekends in this period, orders were turned away because the oven schedule was full." (sales-summary.md, verified)
2. OMR demand is suppressed by delivery price. 25 of 33 OMR respondents gave up ordering.  
   > "Of the 33 OMR respondents, 25 said they had tried to order but gave up because the
  delivery charge to OMR (Rs. 180) was too high." (survey-results.md, verified)
3. There is possible corporate demand on OMR, although it is a small sample.  
   > "Corporate orders came up unprompted: 11 respondents work in OMR IT parks and said their
  offices order desserts for events at least once a month." (survey-results.md, verified)
4. Eggless cakes are the most requested addition, and the OMR competitors don't offer eggless premium cakes. This is a differentiator.  
   > "Neither of them offers eggless versions of their premium cakes." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
5. No comparable home-style bakery is on OMR, while Velachery is crowded.  
   > "No home-style bakery with a following like ours has a kitchen on OMR yet." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
6. Velachery (Option A) faces heavy competition, which weakens it as a site.  
   > "In Velachery there are at least five well-rated bakeries within 3 km of the Vijaya Nagar space." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
7. A Thoraipakkam kitchen would move most OMR deliveries into the cheapest band. Long rides also cause most of the damage complaints.  
   > "From a kitchen in Thoraipakkam, most OMR addresses would be under 5 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
8. Long-distance deliveries cause most damage complaints.  
   > "6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were
on deliveries over 15 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
9. Anna Nagar West (Option C) is the most expensive and does not open a new area, so it is ruled out.  
   > "Notes: easiest to manage, but it does not bring the bakery closer to any new area." (rent-quotes.md, verified)
10. The OMR rent has risen to Rs. 48,000 (the newer figure), and the deposit is still 6 months. That is a Rs. 2.88 lakh deposit.  
   > "the rent for the OMR space is now Rs. 48,000 per month, not the 45,000 in
   your August quote. The deposit is still 6 months." (accountant-note.md, verified)
11. The break-even hurdle is about Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue. That is more than 4 times today's OMR order revenue, which is roughly 41 orders at about Rs. 1,140 each, or about Rs. 47,000.  
   > "a second kitchen needs roughly
   Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue to cover rent, two staff salaries and
   electricity at the OMR space." (accountant-note.md, verified)
12. Setup costs about Rs. 6.5 lakh, and the accountant wants a Rs. 3 lakh cash buffer kept.  
   > "I would keep at least Rs. 3 lakh in the bank as a buffer, so please don't spend all
   the savings on the setup." (accountant-note.md, verified)
13. OMR orders are small and growing only slowly (41 in September), so the 1.9 lakh target depends on new demand, not just on existing OMR orders.  
   > "| September | 325 | 3,70,400 | 41 |" (sales-summary.md, verified)
14. Price sensitivity limits how much revenue each order can bring in.  
   > "Only 9 respondents said they would pay more than Rs. 1,200 for a 1 kg celebration cake." (survey-results.md, verified)

## Where the documents disagree

- Thoraipakkam rent: rent-quotes.md (late August 2026) says Rs. 45,000 per month, but accountant-note.md (28 September 2026) says Rs. 48,000. The accountant's note is newer, so I used Rs. 48,000.
- It's unclear whether the accountant's Rs. 1.9 lakh break-even uses the old Rs. 45,000 rent or the new Rs. 48,000 rent. His note lists the rent rise first, but it doesn't say. If it uses the old rent, the real target is slightly higher.

## Not in the documents

- Amudha's current savings or cash balance. Without it I can't check that the Rs. 6.5 lakh setup, the Rs. 2.88 lakh deposit and the Rs. 3 lakh buffer are affordable.
- Whether the Rs. 6.5 lakh setup estimate includes the security deposit. The note doesn't say.
- Any forecast of how many new OMR orders or corporate orders a Thoraipakkam kitchen would bring in. The survey shows interest but gives no volumes or order values.
- Setup quotes for each site separately. The accountant gives one figure for all three spaces.
- Staff salaries and electricity costs as separate figures, and whether the Anna Nagar kitchen would keep its own staff.
- Whether the landlord of the Thoraipakkam space will hold the space or the rent while Amudha decides, since other tenants are interested.
- The lease length or lock-in terms for any of the three spaces.
- The cost of adding eggless products, such as new recipes or ingredients.
