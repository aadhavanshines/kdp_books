# Should Amudha open a second kitchen, and if so, where?

Yes, conditionally: the evidence favours a second kitchen at Thoraipakkam (OMR), which fixes the Rs. 180 delivery barrier and faces weak, cheap competition. The risk is that break-even needs about Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue versus roughly Rs. 47k of OMR sales today, and the documents don't say whether savings cover the roughly Rs. 9.4 lakh upfront cost plus the Rs. 3 lakh buffer.

## Findings

1. Many OMR customers abandon orders because of the delivery charge, showing unmet demand.  
   > "Of the 33 OMR respondents, 25 said they had tried to order but gave up because the
  delivery charge to OMR (Rs. 180) was too high." (survey-results.md, verified)
2. All OMR orders today are in the most expensive delivery band, and a Thoraipakkam kitchen would cut most deliveries to under 5 km.  
   > "From a kitchen in Thoraipakkam, most OMR addresses would be under 5 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
3. Long-distance delivery causes most damage complaints.  
   > "6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were
on deliveries over 15 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
4. The OMR rent is now higher than the August quote (newer accountant note supersedes the quote).  
   > "the rent for the OMR space is now Rs. 48,000 per month, not the 45,000 in
   your August quote." (accountant-note.md, verified)
5. Setup cost is about Rs. 6.5 lakh for any space.  
   > "Setting up any of the three spaces will cost about Rs. 6.5 lakh" (accountant-note.md, verified)
6. Break-even needs substantial extra revenue.  
   > "a second kitchen needs roughly
   Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue to cover rent, two staff salaries and
   electricity at the OMR space." (accountant-note.md, verified)
7. Accountant wants a cash buffer preserved.  
   > "I would keep at least Rs. 3 lakh in the bank as a buffer" (accountant-note.md, verified)
8. The OMR competitors are cheap, lower-rated cloud bakeries and none has a home-style bakery with a following.  
   > "No home-style bakery with a following like ours has a kitchen on OMR yet." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
9. Competitors don't offer eggless premium cakes, which is the most requested addition.  
   > "Neither of them offers eggless versions of their premium cakes." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
10. Velachery is crowded with competitors.  
   > "In Velachery there are at least five well-rated bakeries within 3 km of the Vijaya
  Nagar space." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
11. The OMR space is closest to OMR customers and near IT parks.  
   > "inside a small commercial complex near two IT parks; 15 to 25 minutes to most
  of OMR." (rent-quotes.md, verified)
12. Anna Nagar West does not expand reach and costs the most.  
   > "easiest to manage, but it does not bring the bakery closer to any new area." (rent-quotes.md, verified)
13. Corporate demand exists in OMR.  
   > "11 respondents work in OMR IT parks and said their
  offices order desserts for events at least once a month." (survey-results.md, verified)
14. Current kitchen is capacity constrained, adding a reason to expand.  
   > "On 14
weekends in this period, orders were turned away because the oven schedule was full." (sales-summary.md, verified)
15. OMR orders are currently small (41 in September), used for my sizing of the revenue gap.  
   > "| September | 325 | 3,70,400 | 41 |" (sales-summary.md, verified)
16. Average order value used for sizing.  
   > "The average order value in September was Rs. 1,140." (sales-summary.md, verified)

## Where the documents disagree

- OMR rent: rent-quotes.md (late August 2026) says Rs. 45,000 per month; accountant-note.md (28 September 2026, newer) says Rs. 48,000. I used Rs. 48,000. The deposit is still 6 months, so it rises to Rs. 2.88 lakh.

## Not in the documents

- Amudha's current savings or cash balance, so affordability of about Rs. 9.4 lakh upfront plus the Rs. 3 lakh buffer cannot be confirmed.
- Staff salary and electricity figures behind the Rs. 1.9 lakh break-even, and whether it uses the Rs. 48,000 or Rs. 45,000 rent.
- How many of the 25 OMR people who gave up would actually order, or how fast OMR volume would grow.
- Whether the Rs. 1.9 lakh break-even is revenue from OMR alone or includes relief of Anna Nagar's weekend capacity.
- The size of the corporate order market or any pricing and volume for it.
- Lease length and terms, or the setup timeline.
