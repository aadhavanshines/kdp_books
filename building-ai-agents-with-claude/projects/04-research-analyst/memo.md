# Should Amudha open a second kitchen, and if so, where?

Yes, probably, and the best location is OMR (Thoraipakkam). That is where demand is being lost, and the kitchen is at capacity. Competition there is weak, and delivery costs would fall sharply. Even at the newer rent of Rs. 48,000, it is a conditional yes: the documents don't say how much savings Amudha has, and OMR would need about Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue (about 167 orders at the September average order value) to pay its way.

## Findings

1. The current kitchen is capacity-constrained, so a second kitchen has a real need behind it.  
   > "The kitchen in Anna Nagar is running at about 90% of its capacity on weekends. On 14 weekends in this period, orders were turned away because the oven schedule was full." (sales-summary.md, verified)
2. OMR demand is suppressed by the Rs. 180 delivery charge. 25 of 33 OMR respondents gave up ordering.  
   > "Of the 33 OMR respondents, 25 said they had tried to order but gave up because the delivery charge to OMR (Rs. 180) was too high." (survey-results.md, verified)
3. Corporate demand exists in OMR IT parks.  
   > "11 respondents work in OMR IT parks and said their offices order desserts for events at least once a month." (survey-results.md, verified)
4. A Thoraipakkam kitchen would move OMR deliveries from the Rs. 180 band to the Rs. 60 band. Long rides also cause most damage complaints.  
   > "From a kitchen in Thoraipakkam, most OMR addresses would be under 5 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
5. Long deliveries cause most damage complaints.  
   > "6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were on deliveries over 15 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
6. No comparable bakery is on OMR, and the existing cloud bakeries compete on cheap cakes without eggless premium options. Eggless is the top customer request.  
   > "No home-style bakery with a following like ours has a kitchen on OMR yet." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
7. Velachery is crowded with competitors, which makes it a weaker choice.  
   > "In Velachery there are at least five well-rated bakeries within 3 km of the Vijaya Nagar space." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
8. Anna Nagar West adds capacity but opens no new market, and it has the highest rent and deposit.  
   > "Notes: easiest to manage, but it does not bring the bakery closer to any new area." (rent-quotes.md, verified)
9. The OMR rent rose after the August quote (Rs. 48,000, not Rs. 45,000). The deposit is still 6 months.  
   > "the rent for the OMR space is now Rs. 48,000 per month, not the 45,000 in your August quote. The deposit is still 6 months." (accountant-note.md, verified)
10. Break-even hurdle for OMR.  
   > "a second kitchen needs roughly Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue to cover rent, two staff salaries and electricity at the OMR space." (accountant-note.md, verified)
11. Setup cost and a cash buffer constrain how the move is financed.  
   > "Setting up any of the three spaces will cost about Rs. 6.5 lakh" (accountant-note.md, verified)
12. Current OMR volume is small, so the hurdle depends on new demand. September had 41 OMR orders out of 325, and the average order value was about Rs. 1,140.  
   > "| September | 325 | 3,70,400 | 41 |" (sales-summary.md, verified)

## Where the documents disagree

- The OMR rent differs between files. rent-quotes.md (late August 2026) says Rs. 45,000 per month, while accountant-note.md (28 September 2026) says Rs. 48,000. I used Rs. 48,000 because the accountant's note is newer. The deposit is 6 months in both, so it is about Rs. 2.88 lakh at the new rent.

## Not in the documents

- Amudha's current savings or available cash. This decides whether the Rs. 6.5 lakh setup, the roughly Rs. 2.88 lakh deposit and the Rs. 3 lakh buffer are affordable.
- How many of the 25 frustrated OMR respondents would actually order, or how much extra revenue OMR would bring. The Rs. 1.9 lakh target can't be checked against the data.
- Staff salaries, electricity and other cost breakdowns behind the Rs. 1.9 lakh figure.
- Lease length and terms, or whether the Thoraipakkam rent can be negotiated or the space might go to another tenant.
- Whether the survey of 126 people, many of them Instagram followers, represents the wider market.
- Corporate order sizes or prices, and whether the 11 IT-park respondents would become regular buyers.
- The cost and effort of adding eggless products, which is the top customer request.
