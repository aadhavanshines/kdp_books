# Competitor Notes

Notes from browsing delivery apps and Instagram in September 2026.

- Two cloud bakeries already deliver on OMR. Both focus on cheap cakes (Rs. 550 to 750
  per kg) and have average ratings of 3.9 and 4.1.
- Neither of them offers eggless versions of their premium cakes.
- No home-style bakery with a following like ours has a kitchen on OMR yet.
- In Velachery there are at least five well-rated bakeries within 3 km of the Vijaya
  Nagar space.
