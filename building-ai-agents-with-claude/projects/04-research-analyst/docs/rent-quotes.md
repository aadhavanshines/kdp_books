# Kitchen Space Quotes

and are approved for food businesses by the landlord.

- Size: 420 sq ft, ground floor
- Rent: Rs. 38,000 per month
- Deposit: 6 months' rent
- Notes: close to the existing Velachery customers; 25 to 35 minutes to most of OMR.

## Option B: OMR (Thoraipakkam)

- Size: 500 sq ft, first floor with a goods lift
- Rent: Rs. 45,000 per month
- Deposit: 6 months' rent
- Notes: inside a small commercial complex near two IT parks; 15 to 25 minutes to most
  of OMR.

## Option C: Anna Nagar West (second unit near the current kitchen)

- Size: 380 sq ft
- Rent: Rs. 52,000 per month
- Deposit: 10 months' rent
- Notes: easiest to manage, but it does not bring the bakery closer to any new area.
