# Delivery Costs

We use a local rider partner. They charge by distance from the kitchen.

| Distance | Charge to us | What we charge the customer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Up to 5 km | Rs. 60 | Rs. 60 |
| 5 to 12 km | Rs. 110 | Rs. 120 |
| 12 to 25 km | Rs. 160 | Rs. 180 |

Anna Nagar to Thoraipakkam is about 21 km, so every OMR order today is in the most
expensive band. From a kitchen in Thoraipakkam, most OMR addresses would be under 5 km.
Cakes over 2 kg travel badly on long rides; 6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were
on deliveries over 15 km.
