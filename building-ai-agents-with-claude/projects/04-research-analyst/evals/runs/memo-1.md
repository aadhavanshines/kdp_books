# Should Amudha open a second kitchen, and if so, where?

Yes, but only if her savings can cover the full cost, and the best location is Thoraipakkam on OMR. OMR has demand that is held back by the Rs. 180 delivery charge, little competition from home-style bakeries, and a landlord rent that is now Rs. 48,000. The documents don't show her savings or whether OMR can reach the roughly Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue needed, so this is a conditional yes.

## Findings

1. The current Anna Nagar kitchen is capacity-constrained, which supports adding a second kitchen.  
   > "The kitchen in Anna Nagar is running at about 90% of its capacity on weekends. On 14 weekends in this period, orders were turned away because the oven schedule was full." (sales-summary.md, verified)
2. OMR demand is suppressed by delivery cost: most OMR respondents tried to order and gave up.  
   > "Of the 33 OMR respondents, 25 said they had tried to order but gave up because the delivery charge to OMR (Rs. 180) was too high." (survey-results.md, verified)
3. OMR has a corporate order opportunity.  
   > "Corporate orders came up unprompted: 11 respondents work in OMR IT parks and said their offices order desserts for events at least once a month." (survey-results.md, verified)
4. A Thoraipakkam kitchen would move most OMR deliveries from the Rs. 180 band to the cheapest band, and would cut long rides, which cause most damage complaints.  
   > "From a kitchen in Thoraipakkam, most OMR addresses would be under 5 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
5. Long-distance delivery causes damage, and the move to OMR would reduce it.  
   > "6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were on deliveries over 15 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
6. The OMR market is open for a home-style bakery with a following. Competitors there are cheap and have no eggless premium cakes, and Amudha's customers want eggless (47 asked).  
   > "No home-style bakery with a following like ours has a kitchen on OMR yet." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
7. Velachery is crowded with competitors, which weakens Option A.  
   > "In Velachery there are at least five well-rated bakeries within 3 km of the Vijaya Nagar space." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
8. Anna Nagar West adds no new area, and it has the highest rent and deposit.  
   > "Notes: easiest to manage, but it does not bring the bakery closer to any new area." (rent-quotes.md, verified)
9. The OMR rent was raised to Rs. 48,000 in the newer accountant note, replacing the August quote of Rs. 45,000.  
   > "the rent for the OMR space is now Rs. 48,000 per month, not the 45,000 in your August quote. The deposit is still 6 months." (accountant-note.md, verified)
10. Setup cost is about Rs. 6.5 lakh at any of the three spaces.  
   > "Setting up any of the three spaces will cost about Rs. 6.5 lakh: a second deck oven, a commercial fridge, a work table, plumbing and the FSSAI licence." (accountant-note.md, verified)
11. The break-even hurdle is about Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue at OMR.  
   > "a second kitchen needs roughly Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue to cover rent, two staff salaries and electricity at the OMR space." (accountant-note.md, verified)
12. A cash buffer of at least Rs. 3 lakh must be kept.  
   > "I would keep at least Rs. 3 lakh in the bank as a buffer, so please don't spend all the savings on the setup." (accountant-note.md, verified)
13. Current OMR volume is small compared with the break-even hurdle: only 41 OMR orders in September, against an average order value of Rs. 1,140. That makes roughly 167 extra orders a month at Rs. 1,140 each, which is my own calculation.  
   > "| September | 325 | 3,70,400 | 41 |" (sales-summary.md, verified)
14. Thoraipakkam is closest to OMR customers (15 to 25 minutes) and sits near two IT parks, which fits the corporate demand.  
   > "inside a small commercial complex near two IT parks; 15 to 25 minutes to most of OMR." (rent-quotes.md, verified)

## Where the documents disagree

- Rent for the OMR (Thoraipakkam) space: rent-quotes.md (late August 2026) says Rs. 45,000 per month, while accountant-note.md (28 September 2026) says Rs. 48,000. The accountant's note is newer, so Rs. 48,000 is used. The deposit is 6 months, so about Rs. 2.88 lakh instead of Rs. 2.70 lakh.

## Not in the documents

- How much cash savings Amudha has. Setup of Rs. 6.5 lakh plus a deposit of about Rs. 2.88 lakh (6 × Rs. 48,000) plus the Rs. 3 lakh buffer means she would need about Rs. 12.4 lakh in total, and that sum is my own arithmetic.
- Whether Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue is achievable. The documents give no forecast of how many new OMR orders or corporate orders would come in.
- The size or value of the corporate orders from OMR IT parks, and how many offices would actually order.
- Whether the Rs. 1.9 lakh hurdle was calculated using the Rs. 48,000 rent or the old Rs. 45,000. The note says only 'at the OMR space'.
- The hurdle figures for Velachery or Anna Nagar West. The accountant gave break-even only for OMR.
- Staffing, the lease length, or the lease terms at any of the three spaces.
- The cost and feasibility of adding more eggless products, and who would make them.
