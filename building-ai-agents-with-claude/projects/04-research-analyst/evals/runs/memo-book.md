# Should Amudha open a second kitchen, and if so, where?

Yes, the documents favour a second kitchen, and Thoraipakkam (OMR) is the best location. It would cut delivery from about 21 km to mostly under 5 km. It would also tap the unmet OMR demand and the capacity limit in Anna Nagar. The catch is that the OMR kitchen needs about Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue, and the documents don't show whether savings can cover the roughly Rs. 9.4 lakh upfront cost and still leave the Rs. 3 lakh buffer.

## Findings

1. The Anna Nagar kitchen is capacity-constrained, and orders were turned away on 14 weekends, so a second kitchen adds capacity.  
   > "The kitchen in Anna Nagar is running at about 90% of its capacity on weekends. On 14 weekends in this period, orders were turned away because the oven schedule was full." (sales-summary.md, verified)
2. OMR demand is suppressed by delivery charges. 25 of 33 OMR respondents gave up ordering.  
   > "Of the 33 OMR respondents, 25 said they had tried to order but gave up because the delivery charge to OMR (Rs. 180) was too high." (survey-results.md, verified)
3. OMR is the second-largest respondent area, and corporate demand exists there.  
   > "Corporate orders came up unprompted: 11 respondents work in OMR IT parks and said their offices order desserts for events at least once a month." (survey-results.md, verified)
4. A Thoraipakkam kitchen would move OMR deliveries from the most expensive band to the cheapest, and it would reduce damage risk.  
   > "Anna Nagar to Thoraipakkam is about 21 km, so every OMR order today is in the most expensive band. From a kitchen in Thoraipakkam, most OMR addresses would be under 5 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
5. Long rides cause most damage complaints.  
   > "6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were on deliveries over 15 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
6. Competition on OMR is weak at the premium end. No similar home bakery is there, and the cloud bakeries sell cheap cakes without eggless premium options.  
   > "No home-style bakery with a following like ours has a kitchen on OMR yet." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
7. Velachery has heavy competition, which weakens Option A.  
   > "In Velachery there are at least five well-rated bakeries within 3 km of the Vijaya Nagar space." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
8. Option C (Anna Nagar West) is the most expensive and brings no new area.  
   > "Notes: easiest to manage, but it does not bring the bakery closer to any new area." (rent-quotes.md, verified)
9. The OMR rent has risen to Rs. 48,000, which is newer than the August quote.  
   > "the rent for the OMR space is now Rs. 48,000 per month, not the 45,000 in your August quote." (accountant-note.md, verified)
10. The OMR kitchen needs about Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue to cover its costs. That is the main risk.  
   > "a second kitchen needs roughly Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue to cover rent, two staff salaries and electricity at the OMR space." (accountant-note.md, verified)
11. Setup costs about Rs. 6.5 lakh, and the accountant wants a Rs. 3 lakh cash buffer kept.  
   > "Setting up any of the three spaces will cost about Rs. 6.5 lakh" (accountant-note.md, verified)
12. The accountant wants the bank buffer protected.  
   > "I would keep at least Rs. 3 lakh in the bank as a buffer, so please don't spend all the savings on the setup." (accountant-note.md, verified)
13. OMR orders today are only about 41 a month, so most of the 1.9 lakh must come from new customers. The average order is Rs. 1,140.  
   > "| September | 325 | 3,70,400 | 41 |" (sales-summary.md, verified)

## Where the documents disagree

- Rent for the OMR (Thoraipakkam) space: rent-quotes.md (August 2026) says Rs. 45,000 per month, but accountant-note.md (28 September 2026) says it is now Rs. 48,000. I used Rs. 48,000 because the accountant's note is newer. The deposit stays at 6 months, which is now Rs. 2.88 lakh.

## Not in the documents

- Amudha's current savings or cash balance. Without it, I can't confirm she can fund the setup (about Rs. 6.5 lakh) plus the deposit (about Rs. 2.88 lakh at Rs. 48,000 rent) and still keep the Rs. 3 lakh buffer.
- Whether the accountant's Rs. 1.9 lakh break-even figure uses the old Rs. 45,000 rent or the new Rs. 48,000. The note doesn't say.
- A forecast of how many new OMR orders the kitchen would win. Today's OMR volume is about 41 orders a month, roughly Rs. 47,000 at the Rs. 1,140 average. The Rs. 1.9 lakh gap is about 165 extra orders a month at that average, which is my own calculation.
- Staff hiring and management plans for running two kitchens, and lease length or terms.
- The cost of adding eggless options, even though 47 respondents asked for them and competitors don't offer them.
- Customer prices on OMR after delivery fees fall, and whether the Rs. 1,200 price ceiling affects the cake mix.
