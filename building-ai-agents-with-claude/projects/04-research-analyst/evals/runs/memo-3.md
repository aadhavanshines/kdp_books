# Should Amudha open a second kitchen, and if so, where?

Yes, but only if Amudha's savings can cover about Rs. 12.4 lakh and still leave the Rs. 3 lakh buffer. If they can, the second kitchen should go in Thoraipakkam (OMR), now quoted at Rs. 48,000 a month. OMR has clear unmet demand, little competition for a home-style bakery, and a kitchen there would take most deliveries from the Rs. 180 band to under 5 km. The risk is the target: about Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue is roughly 165 extra orders a month at the September average order value, and OMR orders today are only about 41 a month.

## Findings

1. OMR is where demand is being lost to price. 25 of 33 OMR respondents tried to order and gave up because of the Rs. 180 delivery charge.  
   > "Of the 33 OMR respondents, 25 said they had tried to order but gave up because the
  delivery charge to OMR (Rs. 180) was too high." (survey-results.md, verified)
2. There is possible corporate demand in OMR IT parks.  
   > "Corporate orders came up unprompted: 11 respondents work in OMR IT parks and said their
  offices order desserts for events at least once a month." (survey-results.md, verified)
3. A Thoraipakkam kitchen would put most OMR addresses in the cheapest delivery band. Today every OMR order is in the most expensive band.  
   > "From a kitchen in Thoraipakkam, most OMR addresses would be under 5 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
4. Long-distance delivery also causes damage. 6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were on deliveries over 15 km.  
   > "6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were
on deliveries over 15 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
5. OMR competitors are cheap cloud bakeries with no eggless premium cakes, and no home-style bakery with a following is on OMR. Eggless was the most requested addition in the survey (47 respondents).  
   > "No home-style bakery with a following like ours has a kitchen on OMR yet." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
6. Velachery is crowded with well-rated bakeries, which weakens Option A despite its lower rent.  
   > "In Velachery there are at least five well-rated bakeries within 3 km of the Vijaya
  Nagar space." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
7. Anna Nagar West is the most expensive option and does not reach a new area. It is also the only option with a 10-month deposit.  
   > "Notes: easiest to manage, but it does not bring the bakery closer to any new area." (rent-quotes.md, verified)
8. The Thoraipakkam rent has risen from the August quote to Rs. 48,000. The deposit is still 6 months, which is Rs. 2.88 lakh.  
   > "the rent for the OMR space is now Rs. 48,000 per month, not the 45,000 in
   your August quote. The deposit is still 6 months." (accountant-note.md, verified)
9. Setup costs about Rs. 6.5 lakh. With the deposit that is about Rs. 9.4 lakh upfront, plus the Rs. 3 lakh buffer the accountant wants kept.  
   > "Setting up any of the three spaces will cost about Rs. 6.5 lakh" (accountant-note.md, verified)
10. The break-even hurdle for the OMR kitchen is about Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue.  
   > "a second kitchen needs roughly
   Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue to cover rent, two staff salaries and
   electricity at the OMR space." (accountant-note.md, verified)
11. The current kitchen is capacity-constrained, so there is some extra demand beyond OMR.  
   > "On 14
weekends in this period, orders were turned away because the oven schedule was full." (sales-summary.md, verified)
12. OMR orders today are small, about 41 a month in September. Moving them to a nearer kitchen would not by itself reach the Rs. 1.9 lakh hurdle.  
   > "| September | 325 | 3,70,400 | 41 |" (sales-summary.md, verified)
13. The average order value used for the break-even estimate is Rs. 1,140.  
   > "The average order value in September was Rs. 1,140." (sales-summary.md, verified)

## Where the documents disagree

- Thoraipakkam rent: rent-quotes.md (late August 2026) says Rs. 45,000 per month, while accountant-note.md (28 September 2026) says Rs. 48,000. The accountant's note is newer, so I used Rs. 48,000.
- The accountant's Rs. 1.9 lakh break-even figure does not say whether it uses the old or new OMR rent. I assumed it reflects the new rent because it appears after the rent update in the same note.

## Not in the documents

- Amudha's current savings or bank balance, so I can't tell whether she can afford about Rs. 9.4 lakh upfront (setup plus deposit) and still keep the Rs. 3 lakh buffer.
- Any updated rent quotes for Velachery or Anna Nagar West after August. The Rs. 38,000 and Rs. 52,000 figures may be out of date.
- How many new orders or how much revenue a Thoraipakkam kitchen would actually generate. The survey shows interest but gives no conversion forecast.
- The cost of the two staff salaries and electricity, so the Rs. 1.9 lakh break-even cannot be checked.
- The lease length or terms, and whether the landlord will negotiate the rent or deposit.
- Whether the oven capacity problem at Anna Nagar would ease once OMR orders move to the new kitchen.
- Whether the cost of eggless recipes or a corporate-order program was considered.
