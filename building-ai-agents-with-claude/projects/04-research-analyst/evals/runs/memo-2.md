# Should Amudha open a second kitchen, and if so, where?

Yes, but only if the cash works. If she opens a second kitchen, it should be the Thoraipakkam (OMR) space, not Velachery or Anna Nagar West. OMR has the clearest unmet demand and the least competition, and a kitchen there would take most OMR deliveries from the Rs. 180 band to the Rs. 60 band. The documents don't show whether OMR can bring in the roughly Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue the accountant says is needed, because OMR sales today are only about 41 orders a month. They also don't give her savings, so I can't confirm she can pay for the setup, the deposit and still keep the Rs. 3 lakh buffer.

## Findings

1. OMR has strong, price-blocked demand: 25 of 33 OMR respondents gave up ordering because of the Rs. 180 delivery charge.  
   > "Of the 33 OMR respondents, 25 said they had tried to order but gave up because the
  delivery charge to OMR (Rs. 180) was too high." (survey-results.md, verified)
2. OMR also offers corporate demand from IT parks.  
   > "Corporate orders came up unprompted: 11 respondents work in OMR IT parks and said their
  offices order desserts for events at least once a month." (survey-results.md, verified)
3. Customers want faster delivery, which a nearer kitchen would give.  
   > "64% said they would order more often if delivery took less than 90 minutes." (survey-results.md, verified)
4. Every OMR order is currently in the most expensive delivery band, and a Thoraipakkam kitchen would put most OMR addresses in the cheapest band (Rs. 60).  
   > "Anna Nagar to Thoraipakkam is about 21 km, so every OMR order today is in the most
expensive band. From a kitchen in Thoraipakkam, most OMR addresses would be under 5 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
5. Long rides cause most of the damage complaints, which a nearer kitchen would reduce.  
   > "6 of the 9 damage complaints this year were
on deliveries over 15 km." (delivery-costs.md, verified)
6. OMR has little competition for a home-style bakery with a following, and the existing cloud bakeries don't offer premium eggless cakes. Eggless was the most requested addition in the survey.  
   > "No home-style bakery with a following like ours has a kitchen on OMR yet." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
7. Velachery is crowded with competitors.  
   > "In Velachery there are at least five well-rated bakeries within 3 km of the Vijaya
  Nagar space." (competitor-notes.md, verified)
8. Anna Nagar West is the most expensive option, has the largest deposit, and brings no new area closer.  
   > "- Notes: easiest to manage, but it does not bring the bakery closer to any new area." (rent-quotes.md, verified)
9. The OMR rent is now Rs. 48,000 per month, up from the Rs. 45,000 quoted in August. The accountant's note is dated 28 September 2026, so it is the newer source. The deposit is still 6 months.  
   > "the rent for the OMR space is now Rs. 48,000 per month, not the 45,000 in
   your August quote. The deposit is still 6 months." (accountant-note.md, verified)
10. Setup costs about Rs. 6.5 lakh at any of the three spaces. A 6-month deposit on OMR at Rs. 48,000 would add Rs. 2.88 lakh (my calculation), so about Rs. 9.4 lakh in upfront cash.  
   > "Setting up any of the three spaces will cost about Rs. 6.5 lakh: a second deck oven,
   a commercial fridge, a work table, plumbing and the FSSAI licence." (accountant-note.md, verified)
11. The break-even bar is high. At the September average order value of Rs. 1,140, Rs. 1.9 lakh is about 167 extra orders a month (my calculation). Current OMR orders are only about 41 a month.  
   > "a second kitchen needs roughly
   Rs. 1.9 lakh of extra monthly revenue to cover rent, two staff salaries and
   electricity at the OMR space." (accountant-note.md, verified)
12. The accountant wants a cash buffer kept.  
   > "I would keep at least Rs. 3 lakh in the bank as a buffer, so please don't spend all
   the savings on the setup." (accountant-note.md, verified)
13. The current kitchen is near capacity and turning orders away, which supports adding capacity.  
   > "The kitchen in Anna Nagar is running at about 90% of its capacity on weekends. On 14
weekends in this period, orders were turned away because the oven schedule was full." (sales-summary.md, verified)
14. OMR orders are small but growing slowly, so the existing base is far below the break-even need.  
   > "| September | 325 | 3,70,400 | 41 |" (sales-summary.md, verified)

## Where the documents disagree

- OMR rent: rent-quotes.md (late August 2026) says Rs. 45,000 per month, but accountant-note.md (28 September 2026) says Rs. 48,000. I used the newer Rs. 48,000.
- The accountant's Rs. 1.9 lakh break-even figure doesn't say whether it uses the old or new OMR rent. I treated it as approximate.

## Not in the documents

- Amudha's current savings or cash balance, so I can't tell whether she can fund about Rs. 9.4 lakh of setup and deposit and still keep the Rs. 3 lakh buffer.
- How many extra orders OMR would actually bring once delivery is cheaper and faster. The survey shows interest but gives no volume forecast.
- Staff salary and electricity figures, which would let me check the Rs. 1.9 lakh break-even.
- Whether the Thoraipakkam landlord will negotiate, or how long the offer stays open given the other interested tenants.
- The cost of keeping the Anna Nagar kitchen running alongside a new one, and how the owner would split her time between the two.
- How much OMR corporate orders would be worth. The survey says only that offices order at least once a month, not how much they spend.
