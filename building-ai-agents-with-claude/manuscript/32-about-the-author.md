# About the Author

**Aadhavan Muthurengan** is an automation and artificial intelligence professional with more than 15 years of experience in the automotive industry.

Throughout a career focused on product development and validation, Aadhavan has designed and built a wide range of in-house tools that automate complex engineering workflows, speed up testing, and help teams deliver reliable, high-quality results, replacing slow, repetitive work with dependable, scalable automation.

That experience shapes this book. In engineering, nothing is trusted until it has been tested, and a system is only as reliable as the checks around it. AI agents are no different. *Building AI Agents with Claude* teaches agents the way an engineer would build them: small, well-defined tools, limits enforced in code, and evaluations that prove the agent works, run after run.

Aadhavan is also the author of *Prompt Engineering Mastery*, a beginner-to-advanced guide to getting better results from AI chatbots, coding assistants, image generators and agents, and *Vibe Coding for Beginners*, a guide to building real software with AI coding tools.

Thank you for reading *Building AI Agents with Claude*. I hope it helps you build agents that people can rely on, and that you enjoy building them as much as I enjoyed writing this book.
