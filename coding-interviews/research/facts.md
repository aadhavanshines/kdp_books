# Research log: interview processes (checked October 2026)

Policy: describe common patterns; cite companies' own pages where they exist; label candidate reports and prep-site claims as such; formats change, so the book tells readers to confirm with their recruiter.

## Official / company sources
- Meta careers (re-checked October 2026: hiring page confirms authorized AI assistant in CoderPad for select roles; no outside AI tools allowed): "Preparing for your software engineering interview at Meta" (metacareers.com/blog/preparing-for-your-software-engineering-interview-at-meta/); "Preparing for your technical screen" (metacareers.com/swe-prep-techscreen/); "Preparing for your full loop interview" (metacareers.com/swe-prep-onsite/); hiring process page (metacareers.com/hiring-process/) says select roles include an authorized AI assistant within CoderPad during technical interviews; practice session in the same tool via Career Profile. Advice: review data structures, big-O, practice without autocomplete, 45 minutes, think aloud, working non-optimal beats unfinished optimal.
- Meta AI-enabled coding round (third-party reports, 2025-2026): ~60 min in CoderPad, replaces one of two coding rounds for some roles; multi-stage task in a small codebase; AI chat cannot edit files; graded on problem solving, code quality, verification of AI output, communication. Treat as reported.
- Amazon (aboutamazon.com "Your complete guide to the Amazon interview process"; "Exploring Amazon's unique interview process"; "What's it like to interview at Amazon?"): 16 Leadership Principles; STAR answers; half LP / half coding and system design is stated on amazon.jobs "SDE III Interview Prep" (re-checked October 2026; level-specific, not universal); two to seven interviewers incl. a Bar Raiser from outside the team; give metrics, use "I", add lessons learned.
- Apple careers interview tips (apple.com/careers/us/interview_tips.html): open-ended questions about past performance; ask about the team; be patient after interview.
- Google: "Preparing for Google technical internship interviews" PDF (services.google.com, 2018): think out loud, clarify, state assumptions, discuss tradeoffs. Hiring committee decides (widely reported; consistent across sources). Google reintroduced at least one in-person round for some engineering roles (Sundar Pichai, reported Feb-Aug 2025; Lex Fridman podcast June 2025) over AI cheating concerns.
- Microsoft (news.microsoft.com/life "How to land a job at Microsoft"): recruiters say fundamental coding skills are among the most challenging parts.

## Candidate reports (label as reported)
- Google loop for SWE: typically 4-5 interviews: coding (2-3), system design for experienced roles, behavioral ("Googleyness and leadership"); independent ratings; hiring committee; team matching.
- SpaceX: reports of C++ emphasis, timed or take-home coding tests, practical problems (parsing, state machines), presentation for some roles; process ~4-8 weeks.
