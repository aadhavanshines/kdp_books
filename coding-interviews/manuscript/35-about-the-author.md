# About the Author

**Aadhavan Muthurengan** is an automation and artificial intelligence professional with more than 15 years of experience in the automotive industry.

Throughout a career focused on product development and validation, Aadhavan has designed and built a wide range of in-house tools that automate complex engineering workflows, speed up testing, and help teams deliver reliable, high-quality results. Much of that work comes down to the skills this book teaches: choosing the right data structure, writing code that handles messy real-world input, and testing it until it can be trusted.

That experience shaped this book. Every problem is original, every solution is tested against a simpler version on thousands of random inputs, and every example session in these pages was produced by running the code. The goal is a book you can trust as much as the code you'll write after reading it.

Aadhavan is also the author of *Prompt Engineering Mastery*, a beginner-to-advanced guide to getting better results from AI; *Vibe Coding for Beginners*, a guide to building real software with AI coding tools; *Building AI Agents with Claude*, a guide to agents that use tools, remember, and get real work done; *Python Programming Hands-On*, a practical, tested introduction to programming; and *How Large Language Models Are Made*, a clear explanation of how today's AI models are built.

Thank you for reading *The Coding Interview Playbook*. Good luck with your interviews: you've done the preparation, now go and show them how you think.
