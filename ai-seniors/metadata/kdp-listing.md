## Description

```html
<b>The friendly, large-print guide to AI for everyone who has ever thought, "That's not for me."</b>

<p>AI assistants can write a warm thank-you note, plan a gentle trip, explain a confusing bill or a word on a doctor's letter, suggest tonight's dinner from what is in the fridge, and teach you almost anything, any time of day. This book shows you exactly how, step by step, in clear large print, with no jargon.</p>

<li>Choose and set up an AI assistant on your phone, tablet or computer, and make the text big and easy to read</li>
<li>Have your first conversation, and ask questions that get great answers</li>
<li>Write emails, cards, letters, speeches and replies to tricky messages</li>
<li>Plan days out and trips that suit your pace, budget and health</li>
<li>Prepare for doctor visits and understand medical words safely</li>
<li>Cook, garden, fix things, learn a language or a new hobby</li>
<li>Find gifts, play games with grandchildren and record your life stories</li>
<li>Understand letters, bills and forms without sharing private details</li>
<li>Spot scams, including new AI voice-cloning tricks, and protect your privacy</li>
<li>Talk instead of type, and use your camera to read small print or identify a plant</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Plus:</b> 100 ready-to-use questions, a plain-English word list, a quick reference page and a one-week practice plan.</p>

<p>Printed in a large, easy-to-read typeface designed for readers with low vision, on big 8.5 x 11 inch pages. <b>A thoughtful Christmas or birthday gift</b> for parents and grandparents who want to enjoy what AI can do, safely and with confidence.</p>
```
