# Introduction

Welcome. If you have heard people talk about "AI" and wondered what all the fuss is about, or whether it is something for you, this book is for you.

An AI assistant is a program you can talk to in plain English. You ask a question or describe what you need, and it writes back an answer, in a few seconds, any time of day or night. It never gets tired of your questions. It never makes you feel silly for asking. And it is far easier to use than most people expect.

Here are a few things readers like you use it for every day:

- Writing a warm thank-you note, or a firm letter to a company
- Planning a trip with plenty of rest stops and step-free hotels
- Understanding a word on a doctor's letter before the next appointment
- Turning what is in the fridge into tonight's dinner
- Learning about birds, history, gardening or a new language
- Checking whether a strange text message might be a scam

You do not need to be "good with computers." If you can send a text message or an email, you can use an AI assistant.

## How This Book Works

This book is written in large print, with short chapters and one idea at a time. Each chapter shows you exactly what to type, what you will see, and what to do next.

Throughout the book you will see shaded boxes like this one. They show the exact words you can type, or say out loud, to your AI assistant:

```text
Please explain what an AI assistant is, in simple words,
as if you were talking to a friend.
```

You can copy these requests word for word, or change them to fit your own life. There is no wrong way to ask.

You will also see boxes like these:

> **Try It:** A short practice task, so you can learn by doing.

> **Safety Tip:** Something important to protect your money, your health or your privacy.

## The Four Parts of the Book

**Part I: Getting Started** explains what an AI assistant is, helps you set one up on your phone, tablet or computer, and walks you through your very first conversation.

**Part II: Everyday Help** is the heart of the book. Each chapter covers one area of life: letters and emails, trips, health questions, the kitchen and garden, hobbies, family, and paperwork.

**Part III: Staying Safe** shows you how to spot scams, including new ones that use AI, how to protect your privacy, and what to do when the AI gets something wrong.

**Part IV: Going Further** shows you how to talk instead of type, how to use your camera with an AI, and gives you a simple one-week practice plan.

At the back of the book you will find **100 ready-to-use questions**, a **plain-English word list**, and a **quick reference page** you can keep beside your phone.

## A Word About Mistakes

You cannot break anything by asking an AI assistant a question. If you do not like an answer, you simply ask again in a different way. If you get lost, you can start a fresh conversation with one tap.

The AI, on the other hand, *can* make mistakes, and this book will show you how to catch them. The golden rule is simple: **use AI for help and ideas, and check anything important** with a doctor, a pharmacist, your bank, or another trusted source.

## A Gift Worth Opening Slowly

Perhaps someone gave you this book as a gift. If so, they were thinking of you, and you might enjoy trying the first few chapters together. Learning side by side is one of the nicest ways to begin.

There is no rush. Read a chapter, try one or two of the examples, and come back tomorrow. In a week, you will be surprised how natural it feels.
