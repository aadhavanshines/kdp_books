# About the Author

Aadhavan Muthurengan writes clear, practical guides to artificial intelligence and technology for everyday readers. His books explain how to use AI tools with confidence, step by step, with an emphasis on safety and real-life examples.

He wrote this book for parents and grandparents who want to enjoy the benefits of AI without the jargon, and for the families who love them.
