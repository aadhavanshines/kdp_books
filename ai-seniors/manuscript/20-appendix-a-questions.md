# Appendix A: 100 Ready-to-Use Questions

Copy any of these into your AI assistant, word for word, or change the words in brackets to fit your life. They are grouped by topic, so you can find what you need quickly.

## Getting to Know Your AI

1. What are ten useful things you can help a retired person with?
2. Please explain how you work, in three simple sentences.
3. What should I never share with you, and why?
4. How do I start a new chat in this app?
5. How can I make the text bigger in this app?
6. Can you speak your answers out loud? How do I turn that on?
7. What is the best way to ask you a question to get a good answer?
8. How sure are you about your last answer?
9. Please explain that again, more simply, in large words.
10. Please summarize our conversation in five bullet points.

## Writing and Messages

11. Write a thank-you note to [name] for [what they did], about four sentences.
12. Write a cheerful birthday message for my [grandson], who is turning [age] and loves [hobby].
13. Write a Christmas card message for an old friend I haven't seen in years.
14. Help me write a polite complaint to [company] about [problem]. Leave blanks for my details.
15. Check the spelling and grammar of my message below, keeping my own words.
16. Make this email warmer and less formal.
17. Help me write a short speech for [occasion], about two minutes long.
18. Help me reply kindly to an invitation I need to decline.
19. Write a short note for the church or club newsletter about [event].
20. Suggest five ways to start a letter to a grandchild.

## Trips and Outings

21. Suggest five relaxing day trips within an hour of [your town], with little walking.
22. Plan a [5]-day trip to [place] for two people in their [70s], with rests every afternoon.
23. Make a packing list for a [7]-day trip to [place] in [month].
24. What questions should I ask a hotel about accessibility before booking?
25. Teach me ten useful phrases in [language] for my trip, with pronunciation.
26. Tell me five interesting stories from the history of [place] to look out for.
27. What should I check before taking a long car journey?
28. How do I keep my medicines safe and on schedule while traveling? Give me questions for my pharmacist.
29. What are common travel scams, and how can I avoid them?
30. Plan a lovely afternoon in [town] for my grandchildren aged [ages].
