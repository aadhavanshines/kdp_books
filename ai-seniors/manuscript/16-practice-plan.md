# Chapter 16: Your One-Week Practice Plan

The best way to become comfortable with an AI assistant is to use it a little every day. Here is a gentle plan for your first week. Each day takes about ten to fifteen minutes. Write the date on the line when you have done each day.

## Day 1: Say Hello

- Open your AI assistant.
- Ask: *"What are three things you can help me with today?"*
- Ask one follow-up question.

## After the First Week

Here are some ideas for keeping up the habit:

- **Morning question.** Each morning, ask one question you have wondered about: a word, a fact, a recipe.
- **Weekly letter.** Use AI to help write a weekly email to family.
- **Monthly project.** Choose one bigger project: a family memory book, a garden plan, or learning fifty words of a language.
- **Teach someone else.** Show a friend how you use it. Teaching is the best way to learn.

## When You Get Stuck

You will sometimes get stuck. Everyone does, including people who use AI every day. When it happens:

1. Start a new chat and ask again, more simply.
2. Tell the AI exactly what you see on your screen.
3. Ask it: *"I'm confused. Can you explain that step again, more slowly?"*
4. Ask a family member or friend to sit with you for ten minutes.
5. Many public libraries and senior centers run free technology help sessions. Ask yours.

## A Final Word

You have learned something many people half your age are still nervous about. You know what an AI assistant is, how to ask it for help, how to use it for writing, planning, learning and family, and, most importantly, how to use it safely.

The rest is simply practice and curiosity. Ask it about the things you love. Ask it the questions you were always too shy to ask anyone. And remember: you are the one in charge. The AI is just a very clever helper.

Enjoy it.
