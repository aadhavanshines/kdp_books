# Chapter 13: Privacy: What Not to Tell an AI

AI assistants are run by companies, and what you type is sent to their computers to produce an answer. Well-known companies take security seriously, but it is wise to treat an AI chat a little like a postcard: fine for most things, not for secrets.

## Never Type These

Keep these out of every AI chat, always:

- **Passwords and PINs**, for anything
- **Codes sent to your phone** by text message
- **Bank account and card numbers**
- **Social Security, Medicare, passport or driver's license numbers**
- **Your full date of birth together with your full name and address**
- **Other people's private details**, such as a friend's medical problems or address

## Fine to Share, in General Terms

You can get excellent help without personal details. Instead of names and numbers, describe the situation:

| Instead of this | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| "I'm Mary Jones, born 3/14/1950, at 12 Oak St." | "I'm a woman in my 70s." |
| "My account number 4417... has a fee" | "My bank charged me a $35 fee." |
| "My neighbor Bob has dementia" | "A neighbor of mine has memory problems." |
| A photo of your whole prescription label | Type just the medicine name |

## Your Chat History

Most assistants keep a history of your conversations, so you can return to them. In the app's **settings** you can usually:

- **Delete** a single conversation, or all of them
- Turn off whether your chats are **used to improve** the company's AI (often called "training" or "improve the model")
- Use a **temporary** or **private** chat that is not saved

To find these, look for **Settings** (often under your name or picture, or a gear icon), then a section called **Data**, **Privacy** or **Data controls**. The exact words vary between apps. If you can't find them, ask the assistant itself:

```text
How do I delete my chat history and turn off using my
chats for training in this app?
```

> **Note:** Ask the AI app about its own settings, then follow its steps. If the steps don't match your screen, tell it what you see.

## Keep Your Account Safe

- **Use a strong, unique password** for your AI account, different from your email and bank passwords.
- **Turn on two-step sign-in** if the app offers it. This means you get a code by text or email when signing in on a new device.
- **Never share** your sign-in codes with anyone who calls or messages you.
- **Sign out** if you use a shared or public computer, such as at the library.

## Shared Devices

If you share a tablet or computer with family members, remember they may be able to see your chat history. That's fine for recipes and trip plans; for anything personal, use a private chat or delete it afterward.

## Children and AI

If grandchildren use your phone or tablet, sit with them when they use an AI assistant. Most AI services have minimum ages in their rules, often 13 or 18, and younger children should only use AI with an adult.

> **Try It:** Open your AI app's settings and find the privacy or data section. You don't have to change anything; just know where it is. If you can't find it, ask the AI how.

## Key Points

- Never type passwords, codes, account numbers or ID numbers.
- Describe situations in general terms; it works just as well.
- Find the privacy settings: delete chats and turn off training if you prefer.
- Protect your account with a unique password and two-step sign-in.
- On shared devices, use private chats or delete personal ones.
