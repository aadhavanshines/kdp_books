# Appendix C: Quick Reference

Keep this page beside your phone or computer.

## Asking Well: The Recipe

```text
I am [who you are or your situation].
Please help me [what you want].
It is for [who or what it is for].
Please [how you want the answer].
```

## Handy Follow-Ups

- "Shorter, please."
- "Explain that more simply."
- "Give me another example."
- "Make it warmer and more personal."
- "Put that in a numbered list."
- "How sure are you? What should I check?"

## The Buttons

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Arrow | Sends your message |
| Pencil or plus | Starts a new chat |
| Three short lines | Opens the menu and your old chats |
| Two squares | Copies the answer |
| Microphone | Speaks instead of typing |
| Paperclip, plus or camera | Adds a photo |

## The Safety Rules

1. **Never** type passwords, PINs, codes, account numbers or ID numbers.
2. **Check** anything important about health, money, law or travel with a trusted source.
3. **In an emergency**, call 911 or your local emergency number first.
4. **Pressure, secrecy and gift cards** mean scam. Hang up and call back on a number you know.
5. **Agree on a family code word** for phone emergencies.
