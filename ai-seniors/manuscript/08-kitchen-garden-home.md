# Chapter 8: Kitchen, Garden and Home

Some of the most satisfying uses of AI are the everyday ones: deciding what to cook, rescuing a struggling plant, or working out how to fix a dripping faucet. This chapter is full of practical examples to try.

## What Can I Cook Tonight?

This is many people's favorite use. Tell the AI what you have, and it suggests what to make.

```text
I have eggs, half a bag of spinach, some cheddar cheese,
two potatoes and an onion. What can I make for one
person for dinner? Something easy, please.
```

Follow-ups make it even better:

- "I don't have an oven, only a stove."
- "Make it softer; I have trouble chewing."
- "How long will it take?"
- "Can I freeze half for tomorrow?"

## Recipes for Special Diets

```text
My doctor has asked me to eat less salt. Please suggest
five tasty low-salt dinners, and some ways to add flavor
without salt.
```

```text
My granddaughter is vegetarian and is visiting on Sunday.
Please suggest a main dish that the whole family will
enjoy, for six people.
```

```text
Please give me a classic apple pie recipe, but for
a smaller pie for two people.
```

> **Note:** If you have a serious allergy or a medical diet, double-check every ingredient on the actual packages. The AI can make mistakes about what foods contain.

## Shopping Lists and Meal Plans

```text
Please plan seven simple dinners for one person for the
week, using cheap, healthy ingredients, with leftovers
used the next day. Then make a shopping list grouped
by supermarket section.
```

The shopping list can be copied into a note on your phone, or printed.

## Measurements and Conversions

```text
This recipe is in grams. Please convert 250 g flour,
100 g butter and 150 g sugar into cups and tablespoons.
```

```text
The recipe says 180°C. What is that in Fahrenheit?
```

## In the Garden

```text
I live in North Carolina. What vegetables can I plant
in raised beds in March? I'd like easy ones for a
beginner.
```

```text
The leaves on my tomato plants are turning yellow from
the bottom up. What might be causing this, and what
should I try first?
```

```text
Please suggest ten colorful flowers for a sunny patio
in pots that are easy to look after and attract
butterflies.
```

In Chapter 15, you will learn how to take a photo of a plant and ask the AI what it is, or what is wrong with it.

## Around the House

AI can walk you through small jobs and help you decide when to call a professional.

```text
My kitchen faucet drips all the time. Please explain the
likely causes in simple words, and tell me which fixes
are easy to do myself and when I should call a plumber.
```

```text
How do I reset my thermostat to a schedule: 68 degrees
from 7 a.m. to 10 p.m., and 62 degrees at night? It is
a programmable model with arrows and a "Program" button.
```

> **Safety Tip:** For anything involving electricity, gas, ladders or heavy lifting, call a qualified professional or ask a family member. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas company's emergency number from outside.

## Getting Your Gadgets to Work

AI assistants are very patient technology helpers.

```text
I have an iPhone. How do I make the ringtone louder and
turn on the flash light when I get a call? Please give
step-by-step instructions in simple words.
```

```text
How do I send a photo from my Android phone to my
daughter by email? Please explain each step.
```

```text
My TV says "No Signal". What simple things can I check
before calling someone?
```

If the steps do not match what you see on your screen, tell the AI exactly what you see: *"I don't have a button called Sound. I see Settings, Display and Battery."* It will adjust its instructions.

> **Try It:** Open your refrigerator or cupboard, list five ingredients, and ask the AI for a recipe. Bonus: actually cook it and tell a friend.

## Key Points

- List what you have; the AI suggests what to cook.
- Ask for recipes that fit your diet, then check labels for allergies.
- Get planting and plant-care advice for your area.
- Use AI to understand small repairs, and call professionals for anything risky.
- Ask for step-by-step help with phones, TVs and gadgets, and say what you actually see.
