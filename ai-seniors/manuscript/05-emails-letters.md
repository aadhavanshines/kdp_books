# Part II: Everyday Help

# Chapter 5: Writing Emails, Letters and Messages

![A smiling man in his seventies at a desk, reading a letter he has just written with help from a laptop.](images/ill-writing-letter.jpg)

Writing is where AI assistants shine. Whether you are staring at a blank card, struggling to word a complaint, or simply want your email to sound just right, the assistant can give you a first draft in seconds. You stay in charge: you read it, change it, and send it in your own name.

## A Thank-You Note

Let's start with something warm.

```text
Please write a short thank-you note to my neighbor Linda,
who brought me soup every day while I had the flu.
Keep it warm and simple, about four sentences.
```

Read the result. Does it sound like you? If not, ask for changes:

```text
Lovely. Please make it a little less formal, and mention
that her chicken soup was the best I've ever had.
```

> **Remember:** The AI's draft is a starting point. Change any words you like. A note in your own voice always means more.

## A Birthday or Holiday Message

```text
Write a cheerful birthday message for my grandson Leo,
who turns 16 tomorrow. He loves skateboarding and
just started learning to drive. Two or three sentences,
with a gentle joke about driving.
```

```text
Write a short Christmas card message for my old friend
Margaret. We worked together for twenty years and now
live far apart. Make it heartfelt but not sad.
```

## A Letter to a Company

AI is very good at writing calm, clear letters that get results. Give it the facts, and say what you want to happen.

```text
Please write a polite but firm email to my internet
company. My internet has stopped working three times
this month, for a whole day each time. I have been a
customer for 12 years. I would like a refund for the
days without service. Keep it short and clear.
```

The assistant will usually leave spaces like **[Your Name]** or **[Account Number]** for you to fill in. That is deliberate: you add your personal details yourself, in your email, not in the AI chat.

> **Safety Tip:** Never type your account numbers, card numbers or passwords into the AI. Let it write the letter with blanks, then fill in the blanks yourself in your email program.

## Replying to a Tricky Message

Sometimes the hard part is knowing what to say. Describe the situation, and ask for help.

```text
My son has invited me to stay for two weeks at Christmas.
I would love to see him, but two weeks is too long for me.
Please help me write a kind reply suggesting one week
instead, without hurting his feelings.
```

## Improving Something You Have Written

You can also paste in your own writing and ask the AI to tidy it up. Copy your text (press and hold, then tap **Copy**), go to the AI, press and hold in the message box, tap **Paste**, and add a request:

```text
Please check the spelling and grammar in my message
below, and make it a little clearer. Keep my own words
as much as possible.

[paste your message here]
```

This is wonderful for club newsletters, church notices, letters to the editor, or anything you want to get just right.

## Longer Writing: Speeches and Tributes

Some writing matters deeply: a toast at a wedding, a few words at an anniversary, or a eulogy for a friend. AI can help you organize your thoughts gently.

```text
I have been asked to say a few words at my best friend's
80th birthday party. We met at school and have been
friends for 70 years. We once got lost on a camping trip
and ate nothing but marshmallows for two days.
Please help me write a two-minute speech that is warm
and funny.
```

For a eulogy or a very personal piece, you might ask the AI for an **outline** rather than finished words, and then write it yourself:

```text
I need to write a short eulogy for my brother, who loved
fishing, his dog, and terrible jokes. Please suggest
an outline with five parts, and some questions I could
ask myself to remember stories about him.
```

## Changing the Tone

You can ask for any tone of voice. Try the same message in different styles to see what you like:

- "more formal" or "more relaxed"
- "warmer" or "more businesslike"
- "funnier" or "more serious"
- "shorter" or "with more detail"
- "suitable for a child to read"

> **Try It:** Think of someone you have been meaning to write to. Ask the AI to help you write a short message to them. Change at least one sentence yourself so it sounds like you, then send it today.

## Key Points

- Give the facts and say what you want; the AI writes the first draft.
- Ask for changes until it sounds like you.
- Leave personal details blank and fill them in yourself.
- Paste your own writing in to have it checked and polished.
- For very personal writing, ask for an outline and use your own words.
