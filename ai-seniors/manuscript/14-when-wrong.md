# Chapter 14: When the AI Gets It Wrong

Even the best AI assistants make mistakes. Sometimes they make up a fact, a date, a book title or a website, and say it as confidently as everything else. Experts call this **hallucinating**. Knowing this, and knowing how to catch it, is one of the most important skills in this book.

## Why It Happens

An AI assistant does not look facts up in a reliable encyclopedia every time. It writes the answer that *sounds* most likely, based on everything it learned. Most of the time that answer is right. Sometimes it is a confident guess, and the guess is wrong.

It is like a well-read friend who never likes to say "I don't know."

## Where Mistakes Are Most Likely

Be especially careful with:

- **Numbers**: prices, dates, doses, phone numbers, statistics
- **Specific facts**: names, addresses, opening hours, laws and rules
- **Very recent events**: news, new products, changed prices
- **Quotations and references**: book titles, articles, websites
- **Local details**: your town's bus times, your county's rules
- **Math with many steps**

It is usually very reliable for: explaining ideas, writing and rewriting, brainstorming, and general knowledge that has been true for a long time.

## Five Ways to Catch a Mistake

**1. Ask how sure it is.**

```text
How confident are you about that? Which parts might
be wrong or out of date?
```

**2. Ask where the information comes from.** Some assistants can search the web and show **sources**: small links under the answer. Tap them to see the original page. If an assistant cannot show sources, ask it where you could check.

```text
Where could I check this on an official website?
```

**3. Ask the question a different way.** If you get two different answers, at least one of them is wrong. Check with a trusted source.

**4. Use common sense.** If an answer seems surprising, too good to be true, or worrying, pause and check.

**5. Check with the real source.** For anything important:

| Topic | Check with |
| --- | --- |
| Health and medicines | Your doctor, nurse or pharmacist |
| Money and taxes | Your bank, an accountant, or the official tax agency |
| Legal matters | A lawyer or legal aid service |
| Travel | The airline, hotel or official tourism website |
| Opening hours and prices | The business's own website, or a phone call |
| Benefits and pensions | The official government agency |

## Telling It About a Mistake

If you know an answer is wrong, simply say so:

```text
That's not right. The library closes at 5 p.m., not
8 p.m. Please update the plan.
```

The assistant will apologize and correct itself within the conversation. (It won't remember the correction in a new chat.)

## A Healthy Attitude

Think of the AI as a helpful assistant who is brilliant at writing and explaining, but who you would never let sign a check on your behalf. It drafts, suggests and explains. **You** decide, and **you** check the things that matter.

> **Try It:** Ask your AI assistant about something you know very well, such as your hometown, your old job or a hobby. See if you can spot anything it gets wrong. It's a good way to get a feel for how much to trust it.

## Key Points

- AI can make up facts and sound completely sure.
- Be careful with numbers, specific facts, recent events and references.
- Ask how sure it is and where to check.
- Confirm important things with the real source.
- You decide; the AI only suggests.
