# About the Author

**Aadhavan Muthurengan** is a technology enthusiast and author who writes practical guides that help people put artificial intelligence to work in their everyday lives and careers. With a focus on clear explanations and hands-on examples, Aadhavan's goal is to make powerful technology accessible to everyone, from curious beginners to experienced builders.
