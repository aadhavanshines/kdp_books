# About the Author

**Aadhavan Muthurengan** is an automation and artificial intelligence professional with more than 15 years of experience in the automotive industry.

Throughout a career focused on product development and validation, Aadhavan has designed and built a wide range of in-house tools that automate complex engineering workflows, speed up testing, and help teams deliver reliable, high-quality results, replacing slow, repetitive work with dependable, scalable automation.

Working where engineering, automation, and AI meet has given Aadhavan a practical, results-driven view of the technology: AI delivers real value only when people know how to direct it clearly, test what it produces, and build reliable systems around it. *Prompt Engineering Mastery* distills that experience into plain explanations, real examples, and ready-to-use templates for anyone, from first-time users to the builders of AI agents.

Thank you for reading *Prompt Engineering Mastery*. I hope this book helps you turn AI from a tool you use occasionally into a capability you apply deliberately and confidently, in your work and in your life.
