# Appendix D: Sources and Further Reading

AI products change faster than any book can. When a feature in this book looks different on your screen, go to the provider's own documentation. These are the official sources used and recommended throughout the book, current as of October 2026. Website structures change, so if a page has moved, search the provider's site for the topic.

## AI Assistants and Models

- **OpenAI (ChatGPT and the OpenAI API):** `help.openai.com` and `platform.openai.com/docs`
- **Anthropic (Claude, Claude Code, and the Claude API):** `support.claude.com`, `docs.claude.com`, and `code.claude.com/docs`
- **Google (Gemini app, Gemini in Workspace, and the Gemini API):** `support.google.com/gemini` and `ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs`
- **Microsoft (Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio):** `support.microsoft.com` (search for "Copilot prompts") and `learn.microsoft.com/microsoft-copilot-studio`
- **GitHub Copilot:** `docs.github.com/copilot`
- **Perplexity:** `perplexity.ai/help-center`
- **Open-weight models:** `huggingface.co/docs` and `ollama.com`

## Agents, Tools, and MCP

- **Model Context Protocol (MCP):** `modelcontextprotocol.io`
- **Chrome DevTools MCP server:** `github.com/ChromeDevTools/chrome-devtools-mcp`
- **Playwright MCP server:** `github.com/microsoft/playwright-mcp`
- **Claude Code subagents and the Claude Agent SDK:** `code.claude.com/docs`
- **OpenAI Agents SDK:** `openai.github.io/openai-agents-python`
- **Google Agent Development Kit (ADK):** `github.com/google/adk-python`

## Images, Video, and Audio

- **Midjourney:** `docs.midjourney.com`
- **Adobe Firefly:** `helpx.adobe.com/firefly`
- **Google Veo:** `deepmind.google/models/veo`
- **Runway:** `help.runwayml.com`
- **Suno:** `help.suno.com`
- **ElevenLabs:** `elevenlabs.io/docs`

## Security, Risk, and Responsible AI

- **OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Model Applications:** `genai.owasp.org`. The standard list of security risks for AI applications, starting with prompt injection.
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework:** `nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework`. A widely used framework for managing AI risks in organizations.

## Publishing with AI

- **Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing content guidelines:** `kdp.amazon.com`, under Help, then Content Guidelines. Includes the rules on disclosing AI-generated text, images, and translations.

## Staying Current

- Read each provider's prompting guide when a new model is released; prompting advice is usually updated with it.
- Re-test your saved prompts (Chapter 22) whenever you switch models or a model is updated.
- Keep your own prompt library and notes. Your tested experience with your own tasks is the most reliable guide you'll have.
