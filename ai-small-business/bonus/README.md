# Bonus Files: Claude AI for Small Business

Free downloads for readers of *Claude AI for Small Business: 100 Ready-to-Use Workflows to Automate Emails, Marketing, Admin and Customer Service, No Coding Needed* by Aadhavan Muthurengan.

To save everything at once, click the green **Code** button and choose **Download ZIP**.

- `Prompt-Library.md` / `.txt`: all 100 prompts, ready to copy.
- `Workflow-Index.csv`: open in any spreadsheet to sort and filter the workflows.
- `templates/`: business profile, brand voice guide, FAQ sheet, reply rules, weekly planner, AI use policy, monthly review and interview scorecard.
