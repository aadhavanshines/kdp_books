# Introduction

If you run a small business, you already know where the time goes. It isn't the work you started the business to do. It is the emails, the follow-ups, the social posts you meant to write last week, the receipts in a shoebox, the job ad you keep putting off, the customer who asked the same question as the last ten customers.

This book is about handing that work to an AI assistant called Claude, carefully and in your own voice, so you get hours back every week.

It is not a beginner's guide to AI. You will not find a history of artificial intelligence here, or a chapter on how language models work. If you have used Claude, or any AI chat assistant, a few times and thought "this could save me time, if only I knew exactly what to ask," this book is the next step. It gives you **100 workflows**: tested, copy-paste prompts for the jobs small businesses actually do, each with an example of what you get back and an honest estimate of the time it saves.

## What a Workflow Is

A workflow in this book is a repeatable job, done with Claude, that ends with something you can use: an email ready to send, a week of social posts, a tidy expense summary, an interview scorecard. Every workflow follows the same pattern:

- **Use it when**: the situation it is made for.
- **Time saved**: a realistic estimate, compared with doing the job by hand.
- **The prompt**: shown in a shaded box like the one below. Copy it, fill in the parts in square brackets, and send it.
- **Example result**: a shortened example of what Claude gives back, for a fictional business, so you know what to expect.
- **Make it better**: one or two ways to improve the result or adapt it.

```
I run [type of business] in [town]. Please write [what you need]
for [who it is for]. Keep it [tone and length].
```

The square brackets are where your details go. Replace `[type of business]` with something like *a family bakery*, and so on. Everything outside the brackets you can usually leave exactly as it is.

## Who This Book Is For

This book is written for owners and managers of small businesses: shops, cafés, salons, trades, studios, consultancies, clinics, agencies, online stores and one-person services. You do not need to write code. Every workflow runs in the ordinary Claude chat window, on a computer or a phone. A few chapters near the end show optional ways to connect Claude to the tools you already use, again without code.

## Your Bonus Files

Every prompt in this book is also in the bonus files, so you never have to retype one. You also get ready-made templates: a business profile, a brand voice guide, a customer FAQ sheet and more. Appendix A explains where to download them and how to use them.

## A Word on Honesty

Claude is very good at drafting, summarizing, organizing and suggesting. It is not your accountant, your lawyer or your HR adviser, and it sometimes states things that are wrong with complete confidence. Throughout the book you will see **Check First** boxes where a workflow touches money, law, hiring or health. Read them. The golden rule is simple: **Claude drafts, you decide.** Nothing goes to a customer, an employee or the tax office until you have read it.

The time-saved estimates are honest guesses for a typical small business, not promises. Your first try at a workflow will take a little longer while you fill in your details; by the third time, most of these jobs take minutes.
