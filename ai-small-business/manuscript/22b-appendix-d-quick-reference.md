# Appendix D: Quick Reference

Keep these pages handy while you work. They collect the habits from Part I (the five parts of a prompt come from Chapter 3) and the fixes from Chapter 19 in one place.

## The Five Parts of a Good Prompt

| Part | What to say | Example |
| Role and context | Who you are and what the business is | "I run a family bakery." |
| The task | Exactly what you want | "Write a reply to this review." |
| The input | The material to work with, pasted after the instructions | The review, your notes, the figures |
| The rules | Tone, length, things to include or avoid | "Warm, under 60 words, no discounts." |
| The format | How the result should look | "The reply only, ready to paste." |

## Ten Follow-Up Phrases That Fix Most Drafts

Instead of starting again, reply with one of these:

1. "Shorter. Half the length, same facts."
2. "Warmer, and less formal. Write it the way I'd say it at the counter."
3. "Firmer, but still polite."
4. "Plainer words. No marketing language."
5. "Use only the facts I gave you. Mark anything missing as [CHECK]."
6. "Give me three different versions so I can choose."
7. "Show your working step by step."
8. "What would a doubtful customer think when they read this?"
9. "Before you answer, ask me any questions you need, one at a time."
10. "Turn this into a checklist I can print."

## Phrases to Add to Any Customer-Facing Prompt

- "Don't promise anything that isn't in my reply rules."
- "Don't give medical, allergy, legal or safety guarantees."
- "Never mention other customers or their details."
- "Sign it as [name]."

## What Never Goes Into a Chat

- Passwords, PINs and security codes
- Full card numbers, bank account numbers and ID numbers
- Customers' or staff members' health information
- Anything your contracts, your professional rules or your AI use policy say must stay private

When in doubt, remove names and numbers, or describe the situation in general terms instead.

## The Five-Second Check

Before anything goes out: **facts, promises, tone, privacy, sense.** If all five are right, send it.
