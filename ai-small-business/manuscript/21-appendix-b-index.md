# Appendix B: Workflow Index

## Marketing

| No. | Workflow | Use it when |

| 3 | Plan a Month of Marketing | Marketing keeps slipping because you never decided what to do this month |
| 4 | Write a Monthly Email Newsletter | You have an email list and want to keep in touch without spending an afternoon on it |
| 5 | Write Product or Service Descriptions | Your website, menu or online shop has thin or missing descriptions |
| 6 | Create a Promotion or Offer | You want to run a sale or offer but are not sure what would work without hurting your margins |
| 7 | Turn Customer Reviews Into Marketing Insights | You have reviews on Google, social media or your shop, but have never sat down to learn from them |
| 8 | Write a Press Release for Local News | You have real news, such as an opening, an anniversary, an award or a charity event, and want local papers or radio to cover it |
| 9 | Write a Website Home Page | Your website's home page is out of date, vague, or was written years ago |
| 10 | Check Your Marketing Before It Goes Out | You have written a flyer, ad, email or post yourself and want a quick second opinion |
