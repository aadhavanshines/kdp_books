## How to Use Them

1. **Fill in the templates first.** Start with the Business Profile, then the Brand Voice Guide. Open each one, replace the blank spaces with your own details and save it.
2. **Add them to a Project.** In Claude, open your Project and add the filled-in templates as files or paste them into the instructions (Chapter 2 and Chapter 15).
3. **Copy prompts from the library.** When you want to use a workflow, find it in the Prompt Library, copy the prompt, paste it into Claude and fill in the brackets.
4. **Save your own versions.** Once a prompt is filled in with your details, save that version in your Project or notes (Chapter 15).

## Using the Workflow Index

Open Workflow-Index.csv in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers. Each row is one workflow, with its chapter, when to use it and the time-saved estimate. Sort or filter it to find workflows for a particular job, or add your own column to mark your favorites.
