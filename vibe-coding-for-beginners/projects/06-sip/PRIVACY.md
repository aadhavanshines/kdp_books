# Sip Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Sip is a water intake tracker. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

## The short version

- Sip does not collect your data.
- Sip does not share your data with anyone.
- Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What Sip stores on your device

- The drinks you log (the amount in millilitres and the time you logged it).
- Your daily goal.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone so it is still there when you close and reopen Sip. Sip deletes drink entries older than 30 days.

## What Sip does not do

- It has no accounts or sign-in.
- It does not connect to the internet or send anything to a server.
- It has no ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party services.
- It does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

## Backups

Your phone's operating system (iOS or Android) may include app data in your own device backup, for example iCloud Backup or Google's Android backup, depending on your device settings. That is handled by your device and your own account. We do not receive or have access to those backups. You control them in your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

Delete the Sip app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings, and everything Sip stored is removed from your device. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing else to delete.

## Children

Sip collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that sends data off your device, we will update this page and describe the change in the app's release notes before it takes effect.
