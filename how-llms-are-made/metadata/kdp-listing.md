```html
<b>How does a chatbot actually work, and what does it take to build one?</b>

<p>This book follows a large language model from start to finish, in plain English, with no math and no programming. You'll see the data it reads, the chips and data centers it is trained on, the electricity and water it uses, and the training that turns a simple next-word predictor into an assistant that can reason, see, draw and act.</p>

<p><b>Every fact is sourced.</b> The cost of training runs, the water used per question, the size of each model: every figure comes from the people who did the work or from careful independent studies, and every chapter ends with its sources and further reading. When the sources disagree, the book says so and explains why.</p>

<p><b>Inside, you will learn:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>How text becomes tokens and meaning becomes numbers</li>
<li>Where training data comes from, how it is cleaned, and the fights over who owns it</li>
<li>How neural networks learn, and how the Transformer works, with everyday analogies</li>
<li>Why scaling laws made companies spend billions</li>
<li>GPUs, TPUs, high-bandwidth memory, AI data centers and the real numbers on energy and water</li>
<li>Post-training, reasoning models, benchmarks, and safety and alignment</li>
<li>What happens in the second after you press Enter, and how models are made smaller and faster</li>
<li>Long context, retrieval and agents that use tools</li>
<li>A guided tour of the open models and the frontier labs</li>
<li>Models that see, generate images and video, listen, speak and make music</li>
<li>What it all means for work, learning, trust and the future</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Credit where it is due.</b> You'll meet the people behind each idea, from Claude Shannon's 1951 guessing game to the eight authors of the Transformer, and an appendix lists who invented what. A timeline, a glossary and fact sheets for the major model families make it a reference you'll come back to.</p>

<p>Twenty-five clear diagrams. Dozens of surprising facts. Current as of October 2026.</p>
```
