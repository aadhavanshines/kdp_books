# Chapter 23: The Frontier Labs and Their Models

A small number of organizations build the most capable models in the world, the "frontier". Their models are mostly closed: we know them through their products, the reports they publish and independent tests, not their weights. This chapter introduces the main labs, their histories and the model families they've produced, as of October 2026. Model names change fast, often several times a year, so the aim is to explain each family's place in the story, not to rank them. Rankings change monthly; the stories last longer.

## OpenAI

**Founded:** December 2015 in San Francisco, as a nonprofit research lab, by a group including **Sam Altman**, **Greg Brockman**, **Ilya Sutskever** and **Elon Musk**, with the mission of ensuring that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. It later created a capped-profit company, attracted billions of dollars from Microsoft, and in 2025 restructured its for-profit arm as a public benefit corporation still controlled by the nonprofit.

**The models:** The **GPT** series ("generative pre-trained transformer") defined the field: GPT-1 (2018), GPT-2 (2019), GPT-3 (2020) and **GPT-4** (March 2023), the first model to pass many professional exams at a high level. **ChatGPT** (November 2022) brought it all to the public. **GPT-4o** (May 2024) added fluent voice conversation and image understanding in one model; the **o-series** (from September 2024) introduced reasoning (Chapter 14). **GPT-5** (August 2025) combined fast answers and deep reasoning in one system that decides how long to think. In 2026, OpenAI rolled out the **GPT-6** family in stages: **GPT-6 Astra**, its flagship, from September 2026, first to vetted partners including cybersecurity defenders, and the smaller, cheaper **GPT-6 Sol** and **Luna**; GPT-6 became ChatGPT's default in October 2026.

**Known for:** Starting the chatbot era, the biggest consumer AI product, and pioneering reasoning models. OpenAI also makes image (GPT Image), voice and coding (Codex) products.

## Anthropic

**Founded:** 2021, by former OpenAI researchers led by siblings **Dario Amodei** (CEO) and **Daniela Amodei** (president), with a strong focus on AI safety. It is structured as a public benefit corporation.

**The models:** **Claude** (March 2023), widely reported to be named after Claude Shannon (Chapter 1), offered a 100,000-token context window within two months of launch. Anthropic introduced its model tiers with **Claude 3** (March 2024): **Haiku** (small and fast), **Sonnet** (balanced) and **Opus** (largest). Claude 3.5 Sonnet (2024) became a favorite of programmers; Claude 3.7 Sonnet (February 2025) was the first hybrid reasoning model; and the Claude 4 and Claude 5 generations pushed further on coding and long-running agentic work. As of October 2026, the current family is **Claude 5.5**: **Claude Opus 5.5**, which Anthropic says performs at the level of its larger Claude Fable 5.1 on most work at lower cost, **Claude Sonnet 5.5** and **Claude Haiku 5.5**. Anthropic also offers larger models, **Claude Fable 5.1** and **Claude Mythos 5.1**, the latter available only to restricted partners.

**Known for:** Safety research (Constitutional AI, the Responsible Scaling Policy, interpretability), strength in coding and agents (Claude Code), and introducing the Model Context Protocol (Chapter 20).

## Google DeepMind

**Founded:** DeepMind was founded in London in 2010 by **Demis Hassabis**, **Shane Legg** and **Mustafa Suleyman**, and bought by Google in 2014. In April 2023, Google merged it with its other AI lab, Google Brain, where the Transformer was invented, to form Google DeepMind, led by Hassabis.

**The models:** Before chatbots, DeepMind was famous for **AlphaGo**, which beat the world Go champion Lee Sedol in 2016, and **AlphaFold**, which solved protein-structure prediction and won Hassabis and John Jumper a share of the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry. Google's first chatbot, **Bard** (March 2023), was followed by **Gemini** (December 2023), designed from the start to be **multimodal**: understanding text, images, audio and video together. Gemini 1.5 (2024) introduced the million-token context window; Gemini 2.5 (2025) added built-in reasoning; **Gemini 3** (November 2025) launched with Deep Think reasoning and an agentic coding platform. In September 2026, Google announced **Gemini 4 Argon**, initially made available to trusted cybersecurity defenders before a wider rollout.

**Known for:** Its own TPU chips, the longest context windows, deep integration with Google Search, Android and Workspace, and leading image and video models (Chapters 25 and 26).

## xAI

**Founded:** 2023, by **Elon Musk**, after his departure from OpenAI's board years earlier. In 2025 it merged with Musk's social network X.

**The models:** **Grok** (November 2023), named after a word from Robert Heinlein's novel *Stranger in a Strange Land* meaning to understand something deeply, was designed with a deliberately irreverent personality and access to real-time posts on X. Grok was trained on the Colossus supercomputer (Chapter 10). **Grok 4** (July 2025) and **Grok 4.7** (September 2026) are the main releases; Grok 5 was announced as in training but not released as of October 2026.

**Known for:** Speed of building infrastructure, integration with X, and controversies over the model's outputs, which led to several public corrections.

## Meta

After years of leading open models with Llama (Chapter 22), Meta formed **Meta Superintelligence Labs** in 2025, hiring researchers from rival labs with widely reported pay packages, and launched the **Muse** family in 2026: **Muse Spark** first as a closed model powering Meta AI across Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, followed by open-weight versions.

## Labs in China

China's AI industry is large and fast-moving. Alongside the open-model makers **DeepSeek**, **Alibaba (Qwen)**, **Moonshot (Kimi)**, **Zhipu (GLM)** and **MiniMax** (Chapter 22), major companies run their own frontier efforts: **ByteDance**, the owner of TikTok, with its **Doubao** models, among the most used chatbots in China, and the **Seedance** video model; **Baidu**, with **ERNIE**, one of the earliest Chinese chatbots (March 2023); and **Tencent**, with **Hunyuan**. Despite export controls on advanced chips (Chapter 9), Chinese models have remained close to the frontier, often by emphasizing efficiency.

## Others at or Near the Frontier

- **Mistral AI** (France), Europe's leading lab (Chapter 22).
- **Microsoft**, OpenAI's biggest partner and investor, which also builds its own **MAI** models.
- **Amazon**, with its **Nova** models and a large investment in Anthropic.
- **Apple**, with on-device and private-cloud models for Apple Intelligence (Chapter 18).
- **Cohere** (Canada), focused on business use, and national AI efforts in countries from India to the United Arab Emirates, Japan and Korea, building models for their own languages.

> **Did You Know?** Many of today's frontier labs trace back to a handful of people. Ilya Sutskever co-created AlexNet with Alex Krizhevsky and Geoffrey Hinton in 2012, co-founded OpenAI, and in 2024 left to found Safe Superintelligence Inc. Dario Amodei led research at OpenAI before co-founding Anthropic. Mustafa Suleyman co-founded DeepMind, later co-founded Inflection AI, and became the head of Microsoft AI. And all eight authors of the Transformer paper eventually left Google, most to found or join AI startups, among them Cohere and Character.AI, though Noam Shazeer later returned to co-lead Gemini.

## How to Keep Up

Because the frontier moves so fast, the best sources are primary ones: the labs' own announcements and **model cards** or **system cards**, which describe a model's abilities, testing and limits, together with independent evaluations from organizations such as Epoch AI, METR, LMArena and government AI institutes. Appendix C lists the main families and where to find their documentation.

## Key Takeaways

- **OpenAI** (2015) created the GPT series and ChatGPT, pioneered reasoning models, and in 2026 rolled out the GPT-6 family.
- **Anthropic** (2021) makes Claude, in Haiku, Sonnet and Opus tiers, with a focus on safety, coding and agents; its current family is Claude 5.5.
- **Google DeepMind** (DeepMind founded 2010, merged 2023) makes Gemini, natively multimodal with very long context, on its own TPUs; Gemini 4 was announced in 2026.
- **xAI** (2023) makes Grok; **Meta** launched its Muse family in 2026; Chinese labs including ByteDance, Baidu, Alibaba and DeepSeek are close to the frontier.
- Names change quickly: rely on model cards and independent evaluations.
