# Vibe Coding Professional Mobile Apps (working title)

Subtitle: Build Android and iOS Apps with Claude Code and Expo: Food Delivery,
E-Commerce, Social and Quick Commerce, End to End, with a Bonus Android TV App

Readers: people who want to ship app-store-quality mobile apps by directing
Claude Code. Some programming helps but isn't required; every prompt, every
result and every screen is real.

## Part I: Foundations
1. Professional Apps, Vibe Coded: what "professional" means (speed, polish,
   states, accessibility, trust), the five apps, how the book was made
2. Your Mobile Workshop: Node, Claude Code, Expo, Android Studio and
   emulators, Xcode on a Mac, Expo Go and development builds, a real phone
3. Directing Claude Code on a Mobile Project: CLAUDE.md, specs, plan mode,
   sessions, reviewing diffs, the verify loop
4. React Native and Expo in One Chapter: components, styles, Expo Router,
   stacks, tabs, modals, sheets, platform differences
5. A Design System Like the Big Apps: tokens, brands, type, spacing,
   elevation, dark mode, the component library (packages/ui)
6. One Backend for Four Apps: the API, seed data, OTP login, JWT, carts,
   orders, live status over SSE, the typed client and React Query

## Part II: Platter, Food Delivery
7. Planning a Food Delivery App: screens, flows, states, the prompt
8. Home, Discovery and Restaurant Pages
9. Cart, Customization and Checkout
10. Live Order Tracking: SSE, the SVG map, timelines

## Part III: Cartly, E-Commerce
11. Planning a Store App
12. Home, Search, Filters and Product Pages
13. Cart, Payments and Orders
14. Returns, Wishlist and Notifications

## Part IV: Lumo, Social Photos
15. Planning a Social App
16. Feed, Stories and the Double-Tap Like
17. Create, Explore, Profile and Messages

## Part V: Minutely, 10-Minute Grocery
18. Speed as a Feature: Planning Quick Commerce
19. Catalog, Cart and the Countdown Delivery

## Part VI: Production
20. Performance: lists, images, startup, animations at 60 fps
21. Accessibility and Localization
22. Testing: unit, component, API, end to end with Maestro
23. Payments for Real: Razorpay, Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay, UPI
24. Push Notifications, Deep Links and Analytics
25. Security and Privacy
26. Builds and Releases: EAS Build, Submit, Update, versions, signing
27. Shipping to Google Play
28. Shipping to the App Store
29. After Launch: crashes, reviews, updates, experiments

## Part VII: Bonus
30. Reelhouse: Building for Android TV (and Apple TV)
