# Appendix E: Resources

These official sources were checked in October 2026. Tools, prices and terms change, so check them before you rely on anything in this book.

## Claude

- **Claude developer documentation:** platform.claude.com/docs. The API, models, structured outputs, tool use and prompt engineering.
- **Models and pricing:** platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/models/overview and platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing.
- **The Claude Console:** platform.claude.com. API keys, usage, costs and spending limits.
- **Agent SDK for Python:** the "Agent SDK" section of the developer documentation, and github.com/anthropics/claude-agent-sdk-python for the source code and changelog.
- **Claude Code documentation:** code.claude.com/docs, including headless mode, permissions, settings, sandboxing and MCP.
- **Commercial Terms of Service:** anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms.
- **Consumer terms update (training choice and retention):** anthropic.com/news/updates-to-our-consumer-terms.
- **Usage Policy:** anthropic.com/legal/aup.

## MCP

- **Model Context Protocol:** modelcontextprotocol.io. The specification and guides.
- **MCP Python SDK:** github.com/modelcontextprotocol/python-sdk, which includes `FastMCP`, used in Chapter 14.

## Python Libraries Used in This Book

- **pandas:** pandas.pydata.org.
- **matplotlib:** matplotlib.org.
- **python-docx:** python-docx.readthedocs.io.
- **openpyxl:** openpyxl.readthedocs.io.
- **pytest:** docs.pytest.org.

## Standards and Methods

The standards mentioned in this book are published by their standards bodies and sold through them and national standards organisations. Read them in full, under your organisation's licence, before relying on any summary, including this book's.

- **ISO 26262**, Road vehicles: Functional safety (iso.org).
- **ISO/SAE 21434**, Road vehicles: Cybersecurity engineering (iso.org).
- **IEC 61508**, Functional safety of electrical, electronic and programmable electronic safety-related systems (iec.ch).
- **Automotive SPICE**, published by the VDA Quality Management Center (vda-qmc.de).
- **AIAG and VDA FMEA Handbook** (aiag.org and vda-qmc.de).
- **IATF 16949** and **ISO 9001**, quality management systems (iatfglobaloversight.org and iso.org).

## Data Protection

- **European Union:** the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
- **India:** the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (meity.gov.in).
