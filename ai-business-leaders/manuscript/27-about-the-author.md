# About the Author

Aadhavan Muthurengan writes clear, practical guides to artificial intelligence for people who need to use it at work. His books explain what AI can do, what it costs and how to use it safely, step by step, with real examples and no hype. He is the author of the AI Skillup series and of *Claude AI for Small Business*.

He wrote this book for the leaders who have been told to "do something with AI" and want to do it in a way their finance team, their staff and their customers can trust.

If this book helped you make a better decision, a short review on Amazon helps other leaders find it. Thank you.
