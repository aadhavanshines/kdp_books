## Template 5: One-Page AI Use Policy

Adapt to your company and have it reviewed by your legal and IT teams.

**[Company] AI Use Policy**

**Why we use AI.** To help our people do better work, serve customers faster and spend less time on repetitive tasks. You remain responsible for your work.

**Approved tools.** You may use: [list approved tools and what each is for]. To request a new tool, contact [name or mailbox]. We aim to reply within [five] working days. Do not use personal AI accounts for company work.

**Never enter into any AI tool** unless that tool is specifically approved for it:

- Customer or employee personal data
- Passwords, bank details or payment card numbers
- Unannounced financial results or forecasts
- Confidential deal, legal or HR information
- Anything a customer or partner has asked us to keep confidential

**Always check** facts, figures, names, prices, dates, and anything that will be sent outside the company or used for a decision. AI can be confidently wrong.

**Be open.** Tell people when content they receive was produced by AI and not reviewed, where they would reasonably want to know. Follow our rules for telling customers when they are dealing with AI.

**Never use AI to make decisions** about hiring, pay, promotion, discipline or dismissal, or to monitor individual employees.

**Report problems.** If AI produces something harmful, wrong in a way that reached a customer, or that exposes confidential information, tell [name or mailbox] the same day.

**Questions?** Contact [name or mailbox]. This policy is reviewed every six months. Last updated: [date].

## Template 6: Tier 2 Review Checklist

From Chapter 15. The project owner completes it; IT and, where relevant, the privacy lead sign it off.

1. What does the AI do, and who checks its output?
2. What data does it use, and is the tool approved for that data?
3. Where is data processed and stored, and does the vendor use it for training?
4. Who can see the AI's outputs and the data it uses?
5. What could go wrong, and what would the effect be?
6. How will errors be noticed and corrected?
7. Will people know they are dealing with AI where that matters?
8. How do users report a problem?
9. How will quality be measured over time?
10. Who owns this use case, and who can switch it off?

## Template 8: Board Update on AI

A one-page quarterly update for your board or owners.

1. **Summary:** one paragraph on progress and the main decisions this quarter.
2. **Portfolio:** the AI register, with status and results for each use case in production.
3. **Results against business cases:** benefits and costs to date, in a table.
4. **Risks and incidents:** any incidents, how they were handled, and changes made.
5. **Regulation:** any relevant changes and our response.
6. **People:** adoption, training, and how employees are responding.
7. **Next quarter:** pilots planned, decisions needed, budget requests.
